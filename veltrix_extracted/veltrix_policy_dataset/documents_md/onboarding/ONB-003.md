# IT and Access Provisioning for New Joiners

## 1. Purpose

A new joiner who spends the first days waiting for a laptop or a password loses momentum and forms a poor first impression. A joiner who receives too much access creates a security risk. This procedure explains how IT, HR and hiring managers work together so that every new employee has the right equipment and the right access on their first day, and no more access than their role needs.

## 2. Scope

The procedure covers laptops and peripherals, default accounts, role-specific access, multi-factor authentication, and access to the internal AI knowledge assistant for new permanent and fixed-term employees. Contractors and interns follow the same steps with the differences described in ONB-005.

## 3. How provisioning is triggered

Provisioning is automated from PeopleHub. When HR Operations marks a candidate as *Joining confirmed* and enters the joining date, base office, department, grade, job title and reporting manager, PeopleHub creates an onboarding ticket in HelpPoint. **Accurate PeopleHub data is essential**: the department and job title determine the joiner's role in the identity provider, and therefore what the AI knowledge assistant and other role-based systems will show them.

## 4. Timeline

| When | Step | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Joining confirmed (normally at least 10 working days before joining) | Onboarding ticket created; laptop allocated from stock | HR Operations, IT |
| 5 working days before joining | Manager raises role-specific access requests | Hiring manager |
| **2 working days before joining** | **Default accounts created**; laptop imaged, encrypted and enrolled | IT Service Desk |
| 1 working day before joining | Final check: laptop, accounts, requested access approved | IT Service Desk |
| Day 1 | Laptop handover, MFA enrolment, password manager set-up | IT Service Desk and joiner |
| Week 1 | Remaining access requests completed | IT, system owners |

For remote joiners, the laptop is couriered so that it **arrives by the Friday before the joining date**, and the day-1 set-up is done by video call.

## 5. Equipment

### 5.1 Standard laptop

Every employee receives a company laptop. The standard configuration has at least 16 GB of memory and a 512 GB encrypted solid-state drive. Engineering and data roles receive a higher-specification model with 32 GB of memory. All laptops are enrolled in device management and run the EDR agent before handover. Laptops are refreshed every 4 years (IT-GDL-001).

### 5.2 Peripherals

A headset is provided to everyone. Monitors, docking stations, keyboards and mice are available at office desks; employees with approved fully remote arrangements may request a monitor for home through HelpPoint with manager approval.

## 6. Accounts and default access

On day 1, every new employee has:
- a company email account and calendar;
- TeamTalk (chat and video);
- PeopleHub (HR self-service);
- ExpenseDesk (expenses and travel);
- HelpPoint (IT support and requests);
- the internal AI knowledge assistant, with access matching the joiner's role;
- the company password manager.

Account names follow the format firstname.lastname, with a number added where a duplicate exists. **Credentials are never sent by email**; the temporary password is handed over in person or by video call on day 1 and must be changed at first sign-in to a passphrase of at least **14 characters** (IT-GDL-002).

## 7. Multi-factor authentication

MFA is mandatory for all systems. On day 1 the IT Service Desk helps the joiner install the company authenticator app on their phone and register a backup method. Engineering, IT and Finance staff who will hold privileged or payment-related access also receive a hardware security key in their first week. Joiners without a suitable smartphone are issued a hardware key on day 1.

## 8. Role-specific access

Access beyond the defaults is requested by the **hiring manager** through HelpPoint at least **5 working days before joining**, following the approval steps in IT-GDL-002: manager approval, then system owner approval, plus Security approval for Restricted data. Common examples:

| Role | Typical additional access |
|---|---|
| Sales and Marketing | LeadLoop CRM, marketing automation |
| Project managers | TrackWise project workspaces |
| Product managers | Product analytics, feedback tracker, TrackWise |
| Finance | Finance system, BuyRight approvals, bank portal (Restricted) |
| Engineering | Source code repositories, non-production cloud environments |
| HR | PeopleHub HR administration (Restricted) |

### 8.1 Access that requires training first

- **Production systems and customer data** are not granted until the joiner completes the Information Security Awareness and Data Protection modules (ONB-001).
- **Source code and engineering environments** additionally require the Secure Development module.
- **Payment systems** require Finance's payment-controls briefing.

## 9. The AI knowledge assistant for new joiners

The assistant is especially useful for new joiners, because it answers questions about policies and processes and shows the source. New joiners should know that:
- it searches only documents their role can access (IT-GDL-005);
- if their role in PeopleHub is wrong, the assistant will show the wrong set of documents, so they should report any mismatch to HR;
- answers should be checked against the cited source before acting on them.

## 10. Common day-1 problems and fixes

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Cannot sign in with the temporary password | Password expired after 24 hours, or typed incorrectly | IT Service Desk issues a new temporary password by video call |
| MFA app not receiving prompts | Phone notifications blocked or wrong account registered | Re-register the authenticator with the Service Desk |
| Laptop asks for a recovery key | Encryption check after a hardware change | Service Desk provides the key after verifying identity |
| Role-specific system shows "no access" | Request not yet approved by the system owner | Manager follows up on the HelpPoint ticket |
| AI assistant shows unexpected documents | Incorrect department or job title in PeopleHub | HR corrects the record; role updates within 1 hour |
| Cannot connect to office Wi-Fi | Device certificate not yet installed | Connect via the guest network and VPN, then raise a P3 ticket |

Joiners should raise problems as soon as they occur rather than working around them; a workaround such as using a colleague's account is a breach of IT-GDL-002.

### 10.1 Returning employees

Employees re-joining Veltrix receive a new account and a newly imaged laptop; old accounts are never re-activated, because their permissions may no longer be appropriate. Historical mailbox data may be restored on request if it is still within the 3-year archive period set in IT-GDL-004.

## 11. Checks and quality measures

IT measures onboarding quality monthly:
- **Day-1 readiness:** percentage of joiners with laptop and default accounts working by 11:00 on day 1 (target 98%).
- **Week-1 access completion:** percentage of requested role-specific access granted by the end of week 1 (target 95%).
Missed targets are reviewed with HR Operations and hiring managers.

## 12. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| HR Operations | Enter accurate joiner details in PeopleHub at least 10 working days before joining |
| Hiring manager | Request role-specific access 5 working days before joining |
| IT Service Desk | Prepare laptop and accounts, enrol MFA, track readiness |
| System owners | Approve access requests promptly |
| Security team | Approve Restricted access; issue hardware keys |
| New joiner | Change temporary password, enrol MFA, complete required training, report access problems |

## 13. Exceptions

If HR confirms a joiner with less than 5 working days' notice, IT treats provisioning as a priority request but cannot guarantee day-1 readiness for role-specific access; default access is always prioritised. Access may never be granted by sharing another employee's account while waiting for approval.

## 14. Worked examples

**Example 1 - late confirmation.** HR confirms a joiner only 3 working days before the start date. IT creates default accounts the next day and prioritises the laptop, but LeadLoop access may follow during week 1.

**Example 2 - wrong department.** A new finance analyst is entered in PeopleHub with the department "Marketing" by mistake. On day 1 the AI assistant shows marketing documents instead of finance documents. The joiner reports it; HR corrects the record, and the role updates in the identity provider.

**Example 3 - production access.** A support engineer requests production database access on day 2. IT holds the request until the Information Security and Data Protection modules are completed.

**Example 4 - remote laptop.** A joiner in Chandigarh starts on Monday 2 March. The laptop must arrive by Friday 27 February.

## 15. Frequently asked questions

**Q: When will my accounts be created?**
A: Two working days before your joining date; you receive your temporary password on day 1.

**Q: Will my password be emailed to me?**
A: No. Credentials are never sent by email.

**Q: What access do I get by default?**
A: Email, TeamTalk, PeopleHub, ExpenseDesk, HelpPoint, the AI knowledge assistant and the password manager.

**Q: Why can't I access production systems yet?**
A: You must first complete the Information Security Awareness and Data Protection modules.

**Q: Who requests access to role-specific systems?**
A: Your hiring manager, at least 5 working days before you join.

**Q: What should I do if the AI assistant seems to show the wrong documents?**
A: Report it to HR; your department or job title in PeopleHub may be wrong.
