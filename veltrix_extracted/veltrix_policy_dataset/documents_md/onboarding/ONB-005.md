# Contractor and Intern Onboarding Procedure

## 1. Purpose

Contractors and interns make an important contribution at Veltrix, from specialist engineers on short projects to students gaining their first work experience. Because they are not permanent employees, their onboarding differs in contracts, access, duration and supervision. This procedure explains how managers engage and onboard them safely and fairly, and how their access is controlled and removed. It is restricted to managers and support functions because it covers commercial and access-control arrangements.

## 2. Scope and definitions

- **Contractor:** an individual who works for Veltrix through a vendor company or as an independent consultant under a services contract. Contractors are not on the Veltrix payroll.
- **Intern:** a student or recent graduate engaged directly by Veltrix for a fixed period of **2 to 6 months** for learning and project work, paid a stipend stated in the internship letter.
- **Sponsor:** the Veltrix manager responsible for the contractor or intern.

Fixed-term employees are not covered here; they follow the full onboarding programme (ONB-001).

## 3. Engaging a contractor

### 3.1 Commercial process

1. The sponsor confirms that the work cannot be done by internal staff within 4 weeks and that budget is available.
2. The sponsor raises a purchase request in **BuyRight**. The Procurement and Purchase Order Procedure (OPS-001) applies: a purchase order is required, and three quotes are needed above Rs 2,00,000 total contract value.
3. Approval follows the Delegation of Authority (OPS-002) based on the **total contract value**, not the monthly rate.
4. Where the contractor will access Confidential or Restricted data, the vendor must pass the **vendor security assessment** before the purchase order is issued.
5. The vendor contract includes confidentiality, data protection and intellectual property clauses; individual contractors also sign the Veltrix **non-disclosure agreement (NDA)** before receiving any access.

### 3.2 Contractor identity

Contractors receive a company account with the suffix *.ext* in the user name (for example *priya.sharma.ext*) and an **orange access badge**, so they can be identified easily. Contractors must not represent themselves to customers as Veltrix employees.

## 4. Engaging an intern

1. Interns are hired through the Recruitment and Selection Policy (HR-POL-004), usually through campus partnerships, with a simplified two-round process: one functional interview and one values interview.
2. The internship letter states the duration (2 to 6 months), stipend, working hours, base office, sponsor and mentor.
3. Each intern is assigned a **mentor** (who may also be the sponsor) and a defined project with clear learning goals.
4. Interns sign the NDA and the intellectual property assignment before starting.

## 5. Access for contractors and interns

### 5.1 Default access

| System | Contractors | Interns |
|---|---|---|
| Company email and TeamTalk | Yes | Yes |
| PeopleHub | No (vendor manages HR) | Yes (stipend, leave) |
| ExpenseDesk | No; expenses through vendor invoice | Yes, for approved business expenses |
| HelpPoint | Yes | Yes |
| AI knowledge assistant | Employee-level documents only | Employee-level documents only |

The AI knowledge assistant gives contractors and interns access only to documents available to the **employee** role, unless the sponsor requests a specific role-based extension through HelpPoint and the owning department approves it (IT-GDL-002).

### 5.2 Additional access

Additional access is requested by the sponsor in HelpPoint and follows the normal approvals. In addition:
- **Restricted data may not be accessed by contractors or interns without CISO approval.**
- Production systems require the same training as for employees (ONB-003).
- Interns do not receive privileged administrator access.

### 5.3 Account expiry

Contractor and intern accounts are created with an **expiry date no later than 6 months** after the start date, or the contract or internship end date if earlier. The sponsor receives a reminder 10 working days before expiry and must renew the account in HelpPoint if the engagement continues, confirming that the access is still needed. Accounts that are not renewed are disabled automatically on the expiry date.

## 6. Training and induction

Contractors and interns complete these modules **within 7 days of starting**, and before access to customer data:
- Code of Conduct and Ethics;
- Information Security Awareness;
- Data Protection and Privacy (CMP-003);
- POSH awareness.
Interns also attend the half-day company induction with new employees (ONB-001).

## 7. Supervision, leave and conduct

- Contractors' working hours and leave are agreed with their vendor and sponsor; Veltrix leave policies do not apply to them (HR-POL-001).
- Interns receive **1 day of paid leave per month** of internship, plus public holidays at their base office.
- Contractors and interns must follow the same standards of conduct as employees (HR-POL-005). Concerns about a contractor are raised by the sponsor with the vendor, and Veltrix may ask for the contractor to be replaced.

### 7.1 Intern projects and evaluation

Each intern project has a written brief agreed in the first week, listing the problem, the expected outputs, the tools and data the intern may use, and the review dates. Interns present their work to the team at the mid-point and at the end of the internship. The mentor completes a short evaluation covering technical skills, learning speed, communication and teamwork; this evaluation is used if the intern is considered for a permanent role. Interns must not be used as a substitute for permanent staff on routine operational work, and they do not handle customer escalations alone.

### 7.2 Intellectual property and data

Everything contractors and interns create in the course of their work for Veltrix, including code, documents, designs and data analyses, belongs to Veltrix under their contract or internship agreement. They may not reuse Veltrix material in other engagements, academic submissions or portfolios without written approval from the sponsor and, for anything containing Confidential data, from the information owner. Interns who need to submit a project report to their college must have the report reviewed by their sponsor so that no Confidential or Restricted information is included.

## 8. Ending the engagement

On the last day, the sponsor confirms in HelpPoint that the engagement has ended. IT disables accounts **within 24 hours** of the end date (the same day if the engagement ends early for conduct reasons), and the contractor or intern returns the laptop and badge. Interns receive an internship certificate and a feedback summary from their mentor. Work products and documents remain with Veltrix.

### 8.1 Interns joining full-time

Interns who perform well may be offered a permanent role. A conversion follows HR-POL-004 with a fast-track process (one final interview), and the intern then completes the full onboarding programme as a new employee, including probation (ONB-004).

## 9. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Sponsor | Justify the engagement, raise requests, supervise work, renew or end access on time |
| Mentor (interns) | Guide learning and project work; give regular feedback |
| Procurement | Run the contractor purchasing process (OPS-001) |
| HR Operations | Issue internship letters, manage intern records |
| IT Service Desk | Create time-limited accounts, disable them at expiry or end date |
| Security team | Run vendor assessments; approve Restricted access with the CISO |
| Finance | Pay vendor invoices against purchase orders; pay intern stipends |

## 10. Exceptions

Engagements of contractors longer than 12 months in total require a review by the department head and HR to confirm that the role should not be a permanent position. Any exception to account expiry or access rules must be approved by the CISO.

## 11. Worked examples

**Example 1 - contract value.** A project manager engages a contractor at Rs 1,50,000 per month for 4 months. The total contract value is Rs 6,00,000, so approval follows the Rs 5,00,001 to Rs 25,00,000 band in OPS-002 (Finance Controller), and three quotes are needed because the value is above Rs 2,00,000.

**Example 2 - account renewal.** A contractor's account expires on 31 August. The sponsor receives a reminder on 17 August and renews it after confirming the work continues.

**Example 3 - Restricted data.** A data contractor needs access to a dataset containing customer identity numbers (Restricted). Access requires CISO approval in addition to the normal approvals.

**Example 4 - intern leave.** An intern on a 3-month internship is entitled to 3 days of paid leave plus public holidays.

## 12. Frequently asked questions

**Q: How long can a contractor or intern account stay active?**
A: Up to 6 months from the start date, or the end date if earlier, unless renewed by the sponsor.

**Q: Can a contractor access Restricted data?**
A: Only with CISO approval.

**Q: What can contractors see in the AI knowledge assistant?**
A: Employee-level documents only, unless an extension is approved.

**Q: How long is an internship?**
A: Between 2 and 6 months.

**Q: How much leave do interns get?**
A: 1 day of paid leave per month of internship, plus public holidays.

**Q: Which approval level applies to a contractor engagement?**
A: The Delegation of Authority band for the total contract value.

**Q: When must contractors complete mandatory training?**
A: Within 7 days of starting and before accessing customer data.
