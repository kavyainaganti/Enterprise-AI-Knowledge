# New Employee Onboarding Programme (First 90 Days)

## 1. Purpose

The first three months in a new job shape how quickly a person becomes productive and whether they decide to stay. Veltrix's onboarding programme gives every new employee a clear, well-supported path from accepting an offer to working confidently and independently. This document explains what happens before the first day, on the first day, in the first week, and through the first 90 days, and who is responsible for each step. It is the main reference for new joiners; managers have a companion guide (ONB-002), and IT provisioning is described in ONB-003.

## 2. Scope

The programme applies to all new permanent and fixed-term employees in every office and in remote roles. Interns and contractors follow a shorter version described in ONB-005. Employees transferring between offices do not repeat the full programme but complete the location-specific parts in section 6.

## 3. Programme at a glance

| Stage | Timing | Key outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-boarding | From offer acceptance to joining day | Documents submitted, accounts created, laptop ready, buddy assigned |
| Day 1 | Joining day (always a Monday) | Welcome, laptop handover, MFA set-up, meet manager and buddy |
| Week 1 | First 5 working days | Company induction, systems set-up, first goals discussed |
| Days 1-30 | First month | Mandatory training completed, benefits enrolled, 30-day check-in |
| Days 31-60 | Second month | Role-specific learning, first independent tasks, 60-day check-in |
| Days 61-90 | Third month | Full ownership of core responsibilities, 90-day review |
| Probation end | Month 6 | Confirmation review under ONB-004 |

## 4. Before the first day (pre-boarding)

### 4.1 Joining dates

New employees join on a **Monday**, or on the next working day if Monday is a public holiday. This allows the weekly induction and IT set-up to run efficiently. Exceptions require the HR business partner's approval.

### 4.2 Documents

After accepting the offer, the joiner receives a PeopleHub pre-boarding link and uploads:
- government photo identity proof and PAN;
- address proof;
- educational certificates for the highest qualification;
- relieving or experience letters from previous employers;
- bank account details for salary;
- a passport-size photograph for the ID card.
Documents are personal data and are handled under the Data Protection and Privacy Policy (CMP-003).

### 4.3 Background verification

Background verification starts once the offer is accepted and must be completed **within 30 days of joining**, as described in the Recruitment and Selection Policy. The joiner can start work while it is in progress.

### 4.4 Accounts and laptop

HR confirms the joining date in PeopleHub, which triggers IT provisioning. **Accounts are created 2 working days before the joining date**, and the laptop is prepared so that it is ready on day 1 (ONB-003). Account details are never sent by email; they are handed over in person or by video call on the first day.

### 4.5 Buddy

The manager assigns an **onboarding buddy** at least one week before the joining date. The buddy is an experienced colleague, not the manager, who helps the joiner with practical questions during the first 90 days. The buddy sends a welcome message before day 1.

### 4.6 Relocation

Joiners who are relocating receive support under the Relocation and Joining Expense Policy (FIN-RMB-003), including temporary accommodation for up to 15 nights.

## 5. Day 1

| Time | Activity | With |
|---|---|---|
| 09:30 | Welcome at reception; ID card and access badge | Facilities and HR Operations |
| 10:00 | Laptop handover, MFA enrolment, password manager set-up | IT Service Desk |
| 11:30 | Meet the manager: role overview, team introductions | Manager |
| 13:00 | Lunch with the buddy and team | Buddy |
| 14:30 | PeopleHub walk-through: profile, leave, payslips | HR Operations |
| 16:00 | Wrap-up with the manager: plan for week 1 | Manager |

Remote joiners receive their laptop by courier before day 1 and complete the same steps by video call.

## 6. Week 1

- **Company induction** (half a day): history, values, products (Veltrix Insight, Veltrix Flow, Veltrix Connect), customers, and how the company is organised.
- **Policy essentials**: hybrid work (HR-POL-002), leave (HR-POL-001), conduct (HR-POL-005), acceptable use of IT (IT-GDL-001) and passwords (IT-GDL-002).
- **Location briefing** for the base office: building access, emergency exits and assembly points, first aiders, canteen and transport.
- **Tools**: TeamTalk, email and calendar, TrackWise and any role-specific systems requested by the manager.
- **First goals conversation** with the manager: what success looks like at 30, 60 and 90 days.

## 7. Days 1-30: settling in

### 7.1 Mandatory training

The following must be completed **within 30 days of joining**:
1. Code of Conduct and Ethics;
2. Information Security Awareness;
3. Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) awareness (CMP-005);
4. Data Protection and Privacy (CMP-003);
5. Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption (CMP-001).
PeopleHub tracks completion. Incomplete mandatory training after 30 days is reported to the manager and HR, and system access may be limited until it is done.

### 7.2 Benefits enrolment

Enrol dependants in group health insurance (a **Rs 5,00,000 family floater**) and confirm nominations for life insurance and provident fund in PeopleHub **within 30 days of joining**. After 30 days, dependants can only be added after a life event such as marriage or birth.

### 7.3 30-day check-in

The manager holds a structured check-in covering what is going well, what is confusing, whether the joiner has the access and tools they need, and progress against 30-day goals. The joiner also completes a short new-joiner survey.

## 8. Days 31-60: building capability

- Role-specific learning, using the learning budget (FIN-RMB-004) where useful.
- Shadowing experienced colleagues in customer meetings, releases or close processes, depending on the role.
- First independent tasks with clear review points.
- **60-day check-in** with the manager, focusing on skills, relationships and any support needed.

## 9. Days 61-90: taking ownership

By day 90 the joiner should own the core responsibilities of the role with normal levels of support. The **90-day review** is a written conversation recorded in PeopleHub that confirms goals for the rest of probation, identifies development needs, and gives early feedback on whether the joiner is on track for confirmation. A second new-joiner survey is sent at day 90.

### 9.1 Improving the programme

The 30-day and 90-day new-joiner surveys ask about the clarity of the role, the usefulness of the induction, readiness of tools and access, and support from the manager and buddy. HR Operations reviews the results every quarter with the IT Service Desk and department heads and publishes the main changes made in response. Joiners can also share suggestions at any time through PeopleHub.

## 10. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| New employee | Submit documents on time, complete mandatory training within 30 days, ask questions, give feedback |
| Manager | Plan the first 90 days, assign a buddy, hold 30, 60 and 90-day check-ins (see ONB-002) |
| Buddy | Answer practical questions, introduce people, check in weekly during the first month |
| HR Operations | Run pre-boarding, PeopleHub set-up, induction and benefits enrolment |
| IT Service Desk | Create accounts, prepare and hand over the laptop, enrol MFA (ONB-003) |
| Facilities | Issue ID card and badge, provide a desk and location briefing |
| HR business partner | Monitor progress, help resolve issues, review survey feedback |

## 11. Exceptions

Senior hires at L6 and above follow an extended programme that adds meetings with each member of the leadership team in the first month. Joiners returning to Veltrix within 12 months of leaving complete only mandatory training and the policy updates briefing. Any other variation needs the HR business partner's agreement.

## 12. Worked examples

**Example 1 - accounts.** Neha's joining date is Monday 6 July. IT creates her accounts by Thursday 2 July, two working days before joining, and her laptop is ready on 6 July.

**Example 2 - missed training.** Arjun has not completed the Data Protection module by day 30. HR notifies his manager, and his access to customer data systems is limited until he completes it.

**Example 3 - adding a spouse to insurance.** Farah joins in May and gets married in December. Because marriage is a life event, she can add her spouse to health insurance in December even though the 30-day joining window has passed.

**Example 4 - remote joiner.** Vikas joins a remote role in Kochi. His laptop is couriered to arrive by the Friday before he joins, and IT completes MFA enrolment with him by video call on Monday morning.

## 13. Frequently asked questions

**Q: What day of the week do new employees join?**
A: Monday, or the next working day if Monday is a public holiday.

**Q: When are my accounts created?**
A: Two working days before your joining date; you receive your details on day 1.

**Q: Which training must I complete in the first 30 days?**
A: Code of Conduct, Information Security, POSH awareness, Data Protection and Anti-Bribery.

**Q: When must I enrol dependants in health insurance?**
A: Within 30 days of joining.

**Q: Who is my onboarding buddy?**
A: An experienced colleague assigned by your manager at least one week before you join.

**Q: What check-ins will I have?**
A: At 30, 60 and 90 days, followed by the confirmation review at the end of probation.

**Q: How long is the probation period?**
A: Six months; see ONB-004.
