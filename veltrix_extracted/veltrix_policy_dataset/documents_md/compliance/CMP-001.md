# Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Policy

## 1. Purpose

Veltrix wins business on the quality of its products and people, never through bribery. Corruption harms customers, distorts competition, exposes individuals to imprisonment and the company to heavy penalties and loss of reputation. This policy sets out the company's **zero-tolerance** position on bribery and corruption, explains what is and is not allowed when giving or receiving anything of value, and describes the controls that apply to third parties, donations, sponsorships and record keeping.

## 2. Scope

The policy applies to everyone who works for or on behalf of Veltrix: employees at all levels, directors, interns, contractors, and third parties such as resellers, implementation partners, agents and consultants when they act for the company. It applies in India and in every other country where Veltrix does business. Where local law is stricter than this policy, the stricter rule applies.

## 3. Definitions

- **Bribe:** anything of value offered, promised, given, requested or accepted to improperly influence a decision or to reward improper performance. "Anything of value" includes cash, gifts, hospitality, travel, jobs or internships for relatives, discounts, loans and favours.
- **Public official:** any person who holds a legislative, administrative or judicial position, works for a government department, regulator or public body, or works for a government-owned or government-controlled company (for example a public sector bank or a state-owned utility), and any political party official or candidate.
- **Facilitation payment:** a small unofficial payment to a public official to speed up a routine action, such as issuing a licence or clearing goods.
- **Third party:** any person or company that acts for Veltrix or with whom Veltrix does business.

## 4. Core rules

1. **Never offer, promise, give, request or accept a bribe**, directly or through anyone else.
2. **Facilitation payments are prohibited**, regardless of amount or local custom. If a payment is demanded under threat to personal safety, pay only what is needed to remain safe, and report it to the Head of Legal and Compliance within 24 hours.
3. **Gifts and hospitality must be reasonable, proportionate and transparent**, and must follow section 5.
4. **Nothing of value may be given to a public official** without prior written approval from the Head of Legal and Compliance (section 6).
5. **Political contributions on behalf of Veltrix are prohibited.**
6. **Charitable donations and sponsorships** require approval under section 8.
7. **Third parties** acting for Veltrix must be checked and contractually bound (section 7).
8. **Books and records** must accurately reflect every transaction (section 9).

## 5. Gifts and hospitality with business partners

The detailed rules on accepting and giving gifts are in the Conflict of Interest and Gifts Policy (CMP-002). In summary:

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Accepting a gift from a business partner | Allowed **up to Rs 3,000** in value; above that, decline or hand it to HR for the gift register |
| Giving a gift to a business partner | Up to Rs 3,000 with manager approval; **above Rs 3,000 needs department-head pre-approval** and an entry in the gift register |
| Business meals | Allowed when reasonable in value and frequency, and when the purpose is genuinely business |
| Hospitality during a live tender or contract negotiation | Not allowed, in either direction, until the decision is made |
| Cash or cash equivalents (vouchers, gift cards) | **Never allowed**, in either direction |

Gifts and hospitality must never be offered or accepted with the intention of influencing a business decision, and must never be secret. If you would be embarrassed to see it reported in a newspaper, it is not acceptable.

## 6. Public officials

Any gift, meal, hospitality, travel or other benefit for a public official, of any value, requires **prior written approval from the Head of Legal and Compliance**. Approval is normally given only for modest items with a clear business purpose, such as refreshments during a meeting at a Veltrix office or a product demonstration attended by officials from a public sector customer. Travel or accommodation for officials is approved only if it is required by a written contract and paid directly to the provider.

Employees must not employ or offer internships to relatives of public officials who are involved in decisions affecting Veltrix without approval from the Head of Legal and Compliance and the CHRO.

## 7. Third parties

Veltrix can be held responsible for bribes paid by its agents, resellers and partners. Before engaging any third party who will interact with customers or public officials on Veltrix's behalf:
1. **Due diligence:** Legal and Compliance checks ownership, reputation, qualifications, links to public officials and any history of corruption. Higher-risk third parties (for example those dealing with government customers) receive enhanced checks.
2. **Contract:** the contract includes anti-bribery clauses, audit rights and the right to terminate for breach.
3. **Fair payment:** fees and commissions must be reasonable for the services provided and paid only to the third party's bank account in its own name, in the country where it operates.
4. **Monitoring:** the business owner watches for red flags throughout the relationship.

Procurement of third parties also follows OPS-001, and approvals follow OPS-002.

### 7.1 Red flags

Report the following to Legal and Compliance before proceeding:
- a request for payment in cash, to a personal account or to an account in a different country;
- unusually high commissions or vague descriptions of services;
- a third party recommended by a public official or customer decision-maker;
- refusal to sign anti-bribery clauses or to disclose ownership;
- requests for donations or sponsorships connected to a pending decision;
- invoices that do not match the work performed.

## 8. Donations and sponsorships

Charitable donations and event sponsorships must be made to registered organisations, for a legitimate purpose, and never to influence a business decision. All donations require approval by the **CFO and CEO**, and must be paid by bank transfer to the organisation's own account. Sponsorships of industry events are approved under the Delegation of Authority (OPS-002) and reviewed by Legal and Compliance if a customer, prospect or public official is involved in organising the event. Corporate social responsibility spending follows the company's CSR plan approved by the Board.

## 9. Books, records and controls

All payments must be recorded accurately and completely, with the correct description and supporting documents. Off-the-books accounts, false invoices and misleading descriptions are prohibited. Finance applies the expense audit in FIN-RMB-001 and card controls in FIN-RMB-005 to detect improper payments, and Internal Audit reviews high-risk areas annually.

## 10. Training and certification

All employees complete anti-bribery training **within 30 days of joining** and **every year** after that. Employees in Sales, Procurement, Finance and anyone dealing with public sector customers complete additional role-specific training. Senior managers (L6 and above) certify annually that they have complied with this policy.

## 11. Reporting concerns

Anyone who suspects bribery or corruption must report it promptly to their manager, to the Head of Legal and Compliance, or anonymously through the Whistleblower and Speak-Up Policy (CMP-004). **Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited** and is itself a disciplinary offence. Employees who refuse to pay or accept a bribe will never be penalised, even if the company loses business as a result.

## 12. Consequences

Breach of this policy is gross misconduct under the Employee Conduct and Disciplinary Policy (HR-POL-005) and may result in termination. Bribery is also a criminal offence for individuals; Veltrix will co-operate with authorities. Third parties who breach the policy may have their contracts terminated.

## 13. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| All employees | Follow this policy, complete training, report concerns |
| Managers | Lead by example, approve gifts within limits, watch for red flags in their teams |
| Head of Legal and Compliance | Own the policy, approve public official benefits, run third-party due diligence, investigate concerns |
| Finance | Maintain accurate records, apply payment controls |
| HR | Maintain the gift register, include the policy in onboarding |
| Board of Directors | Approve the policy and oversee the anti-corruption programme |

## 14. Exceptions

There are **no exceptions** to the prohibition on bribes and facilitation payments. Within the rules on gifts and hospitality, the Head of Legal and Compliance may approve items that exceed the limits in specific cultural contexts (for example a traditional festival gift exchange), provided they are recorded and transparent.

## 15. Worked examples

**Example 1 - customs clearance.** A clearing agent says a demo server will be released from customs faster if Veltrix pays Rs 5,000 "for the officer". This is a facilitation payment and is prohibited. The employee refuses and reports it.

**Example 2 - public sector demo.** A sales manager wants to host officials from a state-owned bank for a product demonstration with lunch at the Hyderabad office. This requires prior written approval from the Head of Legal and Compliance, which is likely to be given for a modest lunch with a clear business purpose.

**Example 3 - tender period.** During an open tender, a prospective customer's evaluator invites the account manager to a concert. Hospitality during a live tender is not allowed; the invitation must be declined.

**Example 4 - reseller commission.** A new reseller asks for a 30% commission paid to an account in another country. The high commission and foreign account are red flags; Legal and Compliance must review before any agreement.

**Example 5 - festival gift.** A vendor sends a box of sweets worth about Rs 1,500 during Diwali. This is below Rs 3,000 and may be accepted and shared with the team.

## 16. Frequently asked questions

**Q: Are small facilitation payments allowed if they are customary?**
A: No. Facilitation payments are prohibited regardless of amount or custom.

**Q: What is the limit for accepting a gift from a business partner?**
A: Rs 3,000; above that, decline it or hand it to HR for the gift register.

**Q: Can I give a gift to a government official?**
A: Only with prior written approval from the Head of Legal and Compliance.

**Q: Who approves charitable donations?**
A: The CFO and the CEO.

**Q: Can Veltrix make political contributions?**
A: No. Political contributions on behalf of Veltrix are prohibited.

**Q: How often is anti-bribery training required?**
A: Within 30 days of joining and every year after that.

**Q: What happens if I refuse to pay a bribe and we lose the deal?**
A: You will never be penalised for refusing to pay or accept a bribe.
