# Data Protection and Privacy Policy

## 1. Purpose

Veltrix handles personal data every day: employee records, candidate CVs, contact details of prospects, and the data that customers' users store in Veltrix Insight, Veltrix Flow and Veltrix Connect. People trust us with this information, and the law requires us to protect it. This policy sets out the principles Veltrix follows when it collects and uses personal data, the rights of individuals, how personal data breaches are handled, and what every employee must do in their daily work.

## 2. Scope

The policy covers all personal data processed by Veltrix, in any format, whether Veltrix decides how the data is used (as a **data fiduciary**, for example for employee and marketing data) or processes it on behalf of a customer (as a **data processor**, for example data inside customer instances of Veltrix products). When acting as a processor, Veltrix follows its contract with the customer in addition to this policy.

## 3. Definitions

- **Personal data:** any data about an individual who can be identified from it, such as a name, email address, phone number, employee ID, IP address or photograph.
- **Sensitive personal data (internal category):** personal data whose misuse could cause significant harm, including financial details, government identity numbers, health information, biometric data, and data about children. Sensitive personal data is classified **Restricted** under IT-GDL-004.
- **Data principal:** the individual to whom personal data relates.
- **Processing:** anything done with personal data, including collecting, storing, using, sharing and deleting it.
- **Personal data breach:** any unauthorised processing, accidental disclosure, loss of access or destruction of personal data.

## 4. Principles

Veltrix processes personal data according to these principles:
1. **Lawful purpose and notice:** personal data is processed only for a lawful purpose, with a clear notice to the individual or on another legal basis permitted by law (for example employment-related purposes).
2. **Consent where required:** where processing relies on consent, the consent is free, specific, informed and unambiguous, and can be withdrawn as easily as it was given.
3. **Purpose limitation:** data collected for one purpose is not used for an unrelated purpose without a new legal basis.
4. **Data minimisation:** collect only what is needed.
5. **Accuracy:** keep data correct and up to date.
6. **Storage limitation:** keep data only as long as needed, following the retention schedule in IT-GDL-004.
7. **Security:** protect data with appropriate technical and organisational measures.
8. **Accountability:** be able to show how these principles are applied.

## 5. Rights of individuals

Individuals whose data Veltrix processes as data fiduciary may:
- request a summary of their personal data and how it is processed;
- request correction, completion or updating of their data;
- request erasure of data that is no longer needed, unless retention is required by law;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- nominate another person to exercise their rights in case of death or incapacity;
- raise a grievance with the Data Protection Officer.

**Requests are logged by the DPO's team and answered within 30 days.** Employees who receive a request (for example an email from a candidate asking for their data to be deleted) must forward it to the DPO's team within 2 working days and must not respond on their own. Requests about data inside a customer's Veltrix instance are passed to the customer, because the customer decides how that data is used.

## 6. Everyday rules for employees

- Use personal data only for the task you are doing and only if your role requires it.
- Store personal data only in approved company systems, never in personal email, personal devices or public AI tools (IT-GDL-001, IT-GDL-005).
- Share personal data only with colleagues who need it, and externally only with approved processors under contract.
- Check recipients before sending emails containing personal data; use secure links for Restricted data.
- Do not download customer production data to laptops. Use anonymised or test data for development and demonstrations.
- Delete personal data according to the retention schedule, and do not keep "just in case" copies.

## 7. Marketing and communications

Marketing emails are sent only to individuals who have opted in. Every marketing email includes a one-click unsubscribe, and **unsubscribe requests are honoured within 48 hours**. Purchased contact lists are not used. Contact data for prospects is kept only in LeadLoop, and exports to spreadsheets need Marketing Operations approval.

## 8. Employee and candidate data

HR processes employee and candidate data for recruitment, employment, payroll, benefits, performance management and legal compliance. Candidate data is kept for 12 months after the hiring decision (HR-POL-004). Employee monitoring for security purposes is described in IT-GDL-001. Health information provided for leave or adjustments is accessible only to HR and, where necessary, the HR business partner, and is never shared with the manager without the employee's consent.

## 9. Vendors and processors

Personal data may be shared with a vendor only if:
- the vendor has passed the vendor security assessment;
- a contract requires the vendor to process the data only on Veltrix's instructions, keep it secure, help with rights requests, report breaches promptly and delete or return the data at the end of the contract;
- for transfers outside India, the destination is not restricted by the Government of India and the contract includes appropriate safeguards.

## 10. Privacy impact assessments

A **privacy impact assessment (PIA)** must be completed and approved by the DPO **before launch** for any new system, product feature or process that:
- processes sensitive personal data at scale;
- monitors individuals systematically;
- uses personal data for automated decisions that significantly affect people;
- transfers personal data to a new country or new vendor category.
Product managers include the PIA in the feature's launch checklist. New uses of AI that involve personal data always require a PIA.

## 11. Personal data breaches

1. Anyone who discovers a possible personal data breach reports it through the IT hotline (IT-GDL-003) **and** informs the Data Protection Officer **within 2 hours of discovery**.
2. The DPO and the Security team assess what data is involved, how many people are affected and the likely harm.
3. The DPO decides, with Legal, on notification to the **Data Protection Board** and to affected individuals as required by law, and to affected customers as required by contract.
4. Containment and recovery follow IT-GDL-003.
5. Every breach, whether or not notified, is recorded in the breach register with the facts, effects and remedial actions.

## 12. Training

All employees complete data protection training **within 30 days of joining** and **every year** after that (ONB-001). Contractors and interns complete it within 7 days of starting (ONB-005). Teams that handle large volumes of personal data (HR, Customer Success, Marketing Operations and Engineering) receive additional training.

## 13. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| All employees | Follow the everyday rules; forward rights requests; report breaches within 2 hours |
| Managers | Make sure team processes follow this policy; support PIAs |
| Data Protection Officer | Own the policy; handle rights requests and grievances; approve PIAs; lead breach assessment and notification |
| Security team | Protect systems; investigate breaches with the DPO |
| Legal | Draft processor contracts; advise on notifications |
| Product managers | Include privacy by design and PIAs in new features |
| HR and Marketing Operations | Apply the specific rules for employee, candidate and marketing data |

## 14. Exceptions

Processing that does not meet this policy may be approved only by the DPO in writing, with a documented legal basis and safeguards, for example a disclosure required by a court order. Requests from law enforcement for personal data must be sent to Legal immediately and must not be answered by individual employees.

## 15. Worked examples

**Example 1 - deletion request.** A former candidate emails a recruiter asking for their CV to be deleted. The recruiter forwards the email to the DPO's team within 2 working days. The DPO's team confirms deletion within 30 days.

**Example 2 - demo data.** A pre-sales engineer wants to use a real customer's dashboard in a demo for another prospect. This is not allowed; the engineer must use the anonymised demo dataset.

**Example 3 - lost spreadsheet.** An employee realises at 15:00 that a spreadsheet with 200 customer users' email addresses was shared with an external link. They report it through the IT hotline and inform the DPO before 17:00.

**Example 4 - new AI feature.** A product team plans an AI feature that summarises support tickets containing personal data. A PIA must be approved by the DPO before launch.

**Example 5 - unsubscribe.** A prospect unsubscribes from a newsletter on Monday morning. The unsubscribe must take effect by Wednesday morning (within 48 hours).

A: Through the IT hotline and to the DPO within 2 hours of discovery.
