# Hybrid Work and Attendance Policy

## 1. Purpose

Veltrix works in a hybrid model: people spend part of the week together in an office and part of the week working remotely. This policy sets the minimum office presence, the hours during which colleagues can rely on each other being available, how attendance is recorded, and the conditions for remote work. The goal is to protect collaboration, learning and team culture while giving employees the flexibility that hybrid work offers.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees attached to the Hyderabad, Bengaluru and Pune offices. Roles that are designated "site-based" in the job description (for example facilities staff, front-desk staff and on-site IT support) follow their shift roster instead of the office-day rules in section 4, but the remaining sections still apply. Employees on an approved fully remote arrangement follow section 6.

## 3. Definitions

- **Office day:** a working day on which the employee is physically present in a Veltrix office for at least 6 hours.
- **Anchor days:** the office days agreed by a team so that members are in the office together.
- **Core hours:** the daily window of **11:00 to 16:00 IST** during which all employees are expected to be available for meetings and collaboration, regardless of location.
- **Remote day:** a working day worked from the employee's registered home address or another approved location in India.
- **Base office:** the office recorded against the employee in PeopleHub.

## 4. Office presence

### 4.1 Minimum office days

Every employee works from their base office **at least 3 days per week**. Teams agree their anchor days with their manager each quarter and record them in the team channel in TeamTalk. Managers should choose anchor days that maximise overlap with the teams they work with most closely.

The 3-day minimum is calculated per calendar week. Weeks that include a public holiday or approved leave are reduced proportionally: for example, an employee who takes 2 days of earned leave in a week is expected in the office for at least 2 of the remaining 3 days.

### 4.2 Core hours

Meetings involving more than one location should be scheduled inside core hours. Outside core hours, employees may organise their working time flexibly provided they complete their contracted hours, meet deadlines and remain reachable for urgent matters agreed with their manager.

### 4.3 Recording attendance

- Office attendance is recorded automatically when the employee's badge is used at the office entrance.
- Remote days must be marked in **PeopleHub by 11:00 IST on the same day**.
- Days that are neither badged nor marked remote, and are not approved leave, are flagged to the manager after 2 working days and may be recorded as leave without pay under HR-POL-001.

### 4.4 Additional remote days

An employee may request additional remote days in a given week through PeopleHub. The manager approves or declines within 1 working day. Reasons that are normally approved include a home repair visit, a short-term care responsibility, or a deep-focus task that benefits from a quiet environment. Requests for more than 2 additional remote days in a month need a short written reason.

## 5. Remote working standards

### 5.1 Location

Remote work is permitted from the employee's registered home address. Working from another location in India (for example a family home) is allowed for up to 20 working days per year with manager approval. **Working from outside India is not permitted without prior written approval from HR, Finance (for tax implications) and IT Security**, because it can create tax, immigration and data-protection obligations for the company.

### 5.2 Workspace and safety

Employees are responsible for a safe, reasonably quiet workspace with a stable internet connection of at least 20 Mbps. After confirmation of probation, employees can claim a **one-time home office allowance of Rs 10,000** through ExpenseDesk for a chair, desk, headset or similar items (see FIN-RMB-001). Receipts are required.

### 5.3 Security when working remotely

- Use only the company-issued laptop for company work.
- Connect through the company VPN on any network other than the home network.
- Lock the screen whenever you step away; laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes.
- Do not print Confidential or Restricted documents at home.
- Do not hold confidential calls in public places where you can be overheard.

These requirements are explained in the Acceptable Use of IT Resources Guideline (IT-GDL-001) and the Data Classification, Handling and Retention Guideline (IT-GDL-004).

## 6. Fully remote and long-term arrangements

### 6.1 When it may be approved

A fully remote arrangement (no regular office days) may be approved where:
- the employee has a medical condition or disability that makes commuting difficult;
- the employee is temporarily caring for a dependant with a serious illness;
- the role was advertised as remote; or
- the employee is relocating and the arrangement bridges the gap until a transfer is completed.

### 6.2 Approval

| Duration | Approvers |
|---|---|
| Up to 2 weeks | Reporting manager |
| More than 2 weeks, up to 6 months | Department head and HR business partner |
| More than 6 months or permanent | Department head, HR business partner and CHRO |

Fully remote arrangements are reviewed at least every 6 months. The company may require attendance at quarterly planning sessions, team events and onboarding activities even under a remote arrangement; reasonable travel costs for such required visits are reimbursed under FIN-RMB-002.

## 7. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Employee | Meet the office-day minimum, mark remote days on time, follow security rules, be available during core hours |
| Reporting manager | Agree anchor days, approve additional remote days, address patterns of non-compliance early |
| Department head | Approve arrangements longer than 2 weeks; make sure team anchor days work across functions |
| HR business partner | Advise on medical and caring arrangements; review fully remote arrangements |
| Facilities | Provide adequate seats and meeting rooms on anchor days (OPS-004) |
| IT | Provide VPN, secure devices and support for remote work |

## 8. Compliance and non-compliance

Managers review attendance data monthly. A first instance of falling below the office-day minimum for two or more weeks in a month results in an informal conversation. Repeated non-compliance without an approved arrangement is handled under the Employee Conduct and Disciplinary Policy (HR-POL-005), starting with a verbal warning. Attendance data is used only for workforce management and is not shared outside HR and the management chain.

## 9. Exceptions

The following are automatic exceptions to the office-day minimum and do not need separate approval:
- the first 2 weeks after returning from maternity leave or long sick leave (a phased return may be agreed);
- days when the base office is closed by Facilities or local authorities;
- business travel days;
- weeks in which the employee has an approved accommodation under the company's disability and inclusion commitments.

Any other exception requires approval under section 6.2.

## 10. Worked examples

**Example 1 - week with leave.** Sunita takes earned leave on Monday and Tuesday. Her office-day expectation for the week falls proportionally: she should be in the office on at least 2 of the remaining 3 working days.

**Example 2 - working from a family home.** Deepak wants to work from his parents' home in Vijayawada for two weeks during a family function. This is within India and within the 20-day annual limit, so his manager can approve it. He must use the VPN, because his parents' Wi-Fi is not his registered home network.

**Example 3 - working from abroad.** Leena plans a holiday in Singapore and wants to work for three days there. This requires prior written approval from HR, Finance and IT Security; her manager cannot approve it alone. Without that approval, the three days must be taken as leave.

**Example 4 - home office allowance.** Mohit joined in January and was confirmed in July. In August he buys an ergonomic chair for Rs 8,400 and a headset for Rs 2,100. He can claim the Rs 10,000 maximum, not the full Rs 10,500, and must upload both receipts.

**Example 5 - unmarked day.** Priyanka forgets to mark a remote day. She has 2 working days to correct it in PeopleHub before her manager is alerted.

## 11. Frequently asked questions

**Q: How many days a week must I be in the office?**
A: At least 3 days per week from your base office, reduced proportionally in weeks with leave or holidays.

**Q: What are the core hours?**
A: 11:00 to 16:00 IST, when everyone should be available for collaboration.

**Q: By when must I mark a remote day?**
A: In PeopleHub by 11:00 IST on the same day.

**Q: Can I work from another city in India for a while?**
A: Yes, for up to 20 working days a year with your manager's approval.

**Q: Can I work from outside India?**
A: Only with prior written approval from HR, Finance and IT Security.

**Q: How much is the home office allowance and when can I claim it?**
A: Rs 10,000, one time, after you are confirmed following probation.

**Q: Who approves a fully remote arrangement for three months?**
A: Your department head and HR business partner.

**Q: Do I need approval to skip office days during business travel?**
A: No. Business travel days are an automatic exception.
