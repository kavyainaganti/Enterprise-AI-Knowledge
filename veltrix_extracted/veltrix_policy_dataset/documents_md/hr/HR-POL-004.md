# Recruitment and Selection Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Veltrix attracts, assesses and hires people. It aims to hire the best available candidate for each role through a process that is fair, consistent, legally compliant and respectful of every candidate's time. The policy is written for hiring managers and interviewers, so it is restricted to people-manager and HR roles; individual contributors who join interview panels receive the interview guidance in section 7 through training.

## 2. Scope

The policy covers permanent, fixed-term and intern hiring in India. Contractors engaged through vendors follow ONB-005 and the procurement rules in OPS-001, although the fairness principles in section 3 still apply.

## 3. Principles

- **Merit:** decisions are based on the skills and behaviours the role requires.
- **Equal opportunity:** no candidate is treated less favourably because of gender, religion, caste, age, disability, marital status, sexual orientation or any other characteristic protected by law or company policy.
- **Consistency:** candidates for the same role are assessed against the same criteria and the same scoring rubric.
- **Transparency:** candidates are told the stages of the process and receive a decision.
- **Confidentiality:** candidate information is personal data and is handled under the Data Protection and Privacy Policy (CMP-003).

## 4. Position approval

No recruitment activity may begin until the position is approved in PeopleHub.

| Request type | Evidence required | Approvers |
|---|---|---|
| Budgeted backfill | Exit confirmed in PeopleHub | Department head |
| Budgeted new position | Position appears in the approved annual workforce plan | Department head and HR business partner |
| Unbudgeted position within the department's cost budget | Business case and Finance confirmation of budget headroom | Department head and CHRO |
| Unbudgeted position exceeding the department's budget | Business case with revenue or risk impact | CHRO and CFO |

The business case states the problem the role solves, the annual cost (band midpoint plus 20% overheads), alternatives considered, and the measurable outcome expected within 6 months.

## 5. Sourcing

### 5.1 Internal candidates first

All approved roles at grade L3 and above are posted on the internal job board for **7 calendar days** before external advertising, unless the CHRO waives this for confidential or urgent hiring. Internal applicants must have been in their current role for at least 12 months and must inform their current manager before the first interview.

### 5.2 External channels

External roles are advertised on the careers page and approved job boards. Employee referrals are encouraged; the referral bonus is **Rs 50,000 for individual contributor roles and Rs 1,00,000 for manager-and-above roles**, paid after the new hire completes 90 days, provided the referrer is still employed. Members of the Talent Acquisition team and the hiring manager for the role are not eligible.

### 5.3 Recruitment agencies

Only agencies on the approved vendor list may be used, under a signed agreement. **Agency fees above 8.33% of the hire's annual CTC require CHRO approval.** Agency engagements are purchases and follow the Procurement and Purchase Order Procedure (OPS-001).

## 6. Selection process

### 6.1 Stages

1. **Screening:** Talent Acquisition reviews applications **within 3 working days** and shortlists against the job description.
2. **Functional interviews:** at least **two technical or functional rounds**.
3. **Values and culture round:** at least one interview focused on how the candidate works with others.
4. **Assignment (optional):** a work sample of no more than 4 hours of candidate effort. Assignments are never used as unpaid work for the company.
5. **Debrief:** the panel meets or submits scores before any decision is discussed.

### 6.2 Panel composition

Panels include at least one person from outside the immediate team. **For roles at L4 and above, at least one woman must be on the interview panel.** Interviewers must have completed interviewer training in the last 24 months.

### 6.3 Relatives and close relationships

A candidate who is a relative or close personal friend of an employee may be hired, but the employee must declare the relationship under the Conflict of Interest and Gifts Policy (CMP-002), may not take part in the selection, and **may not be in the candidate's reporting line** after hiring.

### 6.4 Interview conduct

Interviewers ask questions related only to the requirements of the role. Questions about marriage plans, pregnancy, religion, caste, family background or age are not permitted, even informally. Every candidate for the same role should be asked broadly the same core questions so that answers can be compared. Interviews start on time, and candidates are told at the end what the next step is and when they will hear back. Candidates who need adjustments (for example extra time or an accessible venue) are asked about this when the interview is scheduled.

## 7. Assessment and hiring decision

### 7.1 Scoring rubric

Each interviewer scores the candidate from 1 (weak) to 4 (strong) on: role skills, problem solving, communication, ownership and values fit. Scores and written evidence must be entered in PeopleHub **within 24 hours** of the interview. Interviewers must not discuss their scores with each other before submitting.

### 7.2 Decision rules

| Result | Decision |
|---|---|
| Average score of 3.0 or more, with no score of 1 | Hire |
| Average score from 2.6 to 2.9 | Hold; an additional round may be scheduled |
| Average below 2.6, or any score of 1 in values fit | No hire |

A hiring manager who wants to hire against the rubric's outcome must explain the reason in writing and obtain HR business partner approval.

## 8. Offers

### 8.1 Offer approval

Offers are prepared in PeopleHub with interview scores and the proposed CTC. The proposed CTC is compared with the salary band for the grade, which is maintained by HR.

| Offer position in band | Approvers |
|---|---|
| Minimum to midpoint | Hiring manager and HR business partner |
| Above midpoint up to maximum | CHRO |
| Above band maximum | CHRO and CFO |
| Joining bonus above Rs 2,00,000 | CHRO and CFO |

### 8.2 Offer timeline

The offer letter is released within **1 working day** of final approval. The candidate has **5 working days** to accept. Verbal offers are not binding until the written offer is issued, and recruiters must not promise compensation that has not been approved.

### 8.3 Background verification

Background verification starts after the candidate accepts the offer and must complete **within 30 days of joining**. It covers education, the last two employers, address, identity and criminal record databases. Finance and IT roles with access to Restricted data also receive a credit check. An adverse finding is reviewed by the CHRO, and misrepresentation may lead to withdrawal of the offer or termination.

## 9. Targets

- **Time to hire** (position approval to offer acceptance): **45 days**.
- Offer acceptance rate: 80% or higher.
- Candidate feedback score: 4 out of 5 or higher.

## 10. Candidate data

Candidate CVs and interview notes are stored only in PeopleHub. Unsuccessful candidates' data is retained for 12 months for future roles unless the candidate asks for deletion earlier; it is then deleted. Interview notes must be professional and factual, because candidates may request access to their personal data.

## 11. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Hiring manager | Raise the position request, define the role, chair the debrief, make the hire decision within the rubric |
| Interviewer | Complete training, assess fairly, submit scores within 24 hours |
| Talent Acquisition | Advertise, screen, schedule, manage agencies, prepare offers |
| HR business partner | Approve offers within band, advise on exceptions |
| CHRO | Approve above-midpoint offers, agency-fee exceptions and unbudgeted roles |
| CFO | Co-approve offers above band maximum, large joining bonuses and unbudgeted roles exceeding budget |

## 12. Exceptions

Campus hiring follows a separate annual plan approved by the CHRO, with standard offers for each grade. Re-hiring a former employee within 12 months of exit requires the approval of the department head and the HR business partner, and is not allowed for employees who left during a disciplinary process.

## 13. Worked examples

**Example 1 - unbudgeted role.** The Marketing team wants an extra content writer not in the workforce plan. Finance confirms the role fits within Marketing's cost budget, so the department head and CHRO approve it; the CFO is not needed.

**Example 2 - scoring outcome.** A candidate averages 3.3 but one interviewer scored values fit at 1. Under the decision rules the outcome is no hire.

**Example 3 - offer above midpoint.** The proposed CTC for an L4 candidate is above the band midpoint but below the maximum. The offer needs CHRO approval before release.

**Example 4 - agency fee.** An agency proposes a 10% fee for a senior hire. Because this exceeds 8.33% of CTC, the CHRO must approve it.

## 14. Frequently asked questions

**Q: How long is a role posted internally before external advertising?**
A: 7 calendar days for roles at L3 and above, unless the CHRO waives it.

**Q: How quickly must interviewers submit scores?**
A: Within 24 hours of the interview, in PeopleHub.

**Q: What score is needed to hire?**
A: An average of 3.0 or more with no score of 1.

**Q: Who approves an offer above the band maximum?**
A: The CHRO and the CFO.

**Q: How long does a candidate have to accept an offer?**
A: 5 working days.

**Q: What is the time-to-hire target?**
A: 45 days from position approval to offer acceptance.

**Q: Can my relative work in my team?**
A: They can be hired if you declare the relationship and stay out of the selection, but they cannot be in your reporting line.
