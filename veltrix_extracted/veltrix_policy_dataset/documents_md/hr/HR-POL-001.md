# Leave and Holidays Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how much paid time off employees of Veltrix Systems Pvt. Ltd. ("Veltrix" or "the company") receive, how leave is requested and approved, and what happens to unused leave. Time away from work is essential for health, family life and sustained performance, and the company expects every employee to take their leave rather than accumulate it indefinitely. The policy also gives managers a consistent basis for approving or declining requests so that decisions are fair across teams and locations.

## 2. Scope

The policy applies to all permanent employees working from the Hyderabad headquarters, the Bengaluru and Pune offices, and approved remote locations within India. Fixed-term employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis for the duration of their contract. Interns, consultants and contractors are not covered; their time off is governed by their engagement letter (see ONB-005). Where a state law gives an employee a more generous entitlement than this policy, the state law prevails for that employee.

## 3. Definitions

- **Leave year** means the company's financial year, which runs from 1 April to 31 March.
- **Working day** means Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays declared in the company holiday calendar.
- **Earned leave (EL)** is planned paid leave that accrues with service.
- **Casual leave (CL)** is short paid leave for unplanned personal matters.
- **Sick leave (SL)** is paid leave when an employee is unwell or needs medical treatment.
- **Leave without pay (LWP)** is approved or unapproved absence for which salary is not paid.
- **Reporting manager** is the manager recorded in PeopleHub, the company's HR management system.

## 4. Leave entitlements

### 4.1 Earned leave

Each employee receives **18 days of earned leave** per leave year. Earned leave accrues at **1.5 days per month** of service, credited on the first working day of each month. Employees who join mid-month receive a proportionate credit for that month, rounded to the nearest half day.

Unused earned leave may be **carried forward up to a maximum balance of 30 days**. Any balance above 30 days on 31 March lapses automatically. At the time of exit, up to **15 days of earned leave** may be encashed at the basic salary rate; balances above 15 days lapse. Earned leave cannot be encashed while in service.

### 4.2 Casual leave

Each employee receives **8 days of casual leave** per leave year, credited in full on 1 April (pro-rata for joiners). Casual leave cannot be carried forward and cannot be encashed. A single spell of casual leave may not exceed **3 consecutive working days**; longer absences must be taken as earned leave.

### 4.3 Sick leave

Each employee receives **10 days of sick leave** per leave year. A **medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days**. Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed. Where an employee exhausts sick leave because of a serious illness, the CHRO may approve additional paid sick leave of up to 15 days after reviewing medical documentation, before earned leave or LWP is used.

### 4.4 Parental leave

- **Maternity leave:** eligible employees receive **26 weeks of paid maternity leave**, consistent with the Maternity Benefit Act. Up to 8 weeks may be taken before the expected date of delivery. Employees adopting a child below three months of age, and commissioning mothers, receive 12 weeks of paid leave from the date the child is handed over.
- **Paternity leave:** employees receive **10 working days of paid paternity leave**, which must be taken **within 3 months of the birth or adoption**. It may be taken in up to two blocks.
- After maternity leave, employees may request a phased return of up to 4 weeks at reduced hours under the Hybrid Work and Attendance Policy (HR-POL-002).

### 4.5 Other paid leave

| Leave type | Entitlement | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Bereavement leave | 5 working days per occurrence | Death of a spouse, child, parent, sibling or parent-in-law |
| Marriage leave | 5 working days, once during employment | Must be taken within 30 days of the marriage date |
| Voting leave | Time needed to vote on polling day | Where polling day is not already a holiday in that location |
| Jury or court attendance | As required | On production of a summons |

### 4.6 Public and optional holidays

The company observes **12 fixed public holidays** and allows each employee to choose **2 optional holidays** per calendar year from a published list. The holiday calendar is published in PeopleHub every December. Because the three offices are in different states, a small number of holidays differ by location; employees follow the calendar of their base office, not the office of their manager.

## 5. Requesting and approving leave

### 5.1 How to apply

1. Log in to **PeopleHub** and open *Leave > Apply*.
2. Select the leave type and dates. PeopleHub shows the remaining balance and blocks requests that exceed it.
3. Add a reason where required. A reason is optional for earned leave but mandatory for LWP and for sick leave longer than 2 days.
4. Submit the request. The reporting manager receives an email and a PeopleHub task.

### 5.2 Notice requirements

- **Earned leave of more than 3 working days must be applied for at least 7 calendar days in advance.** Shorter earned leave should be applied for as early as practical.
- Casual leave should be requested before it starts where possible; if not, it must be recorded within 2 working days of return.
- Sick leave may be applied for on the day of absence or within 2 working days of returning to work.

### 5.3 Manager decision

The reporting manager must approve or decline a request **within 2 working days**. A request not acted upon within 2 working days escalates automatically to the skip-level manager. Managers may decline earned leave only for a documented business reason, such as a product release, month-end close or a shortage of cover, and should propose alternative dates. Sick leave, bereavement leave and statutory parental leave cannot be declined.

### 5.4 Cancelling or changing leave

Employees may cancel approved leave at any time before it starts. Cancelling leave that has already started, or shortening it, requires manager approval. If the company asks an employee to cancel approved leave for business reasons, non-refundable travel and booking costs are reimbursed under the Expense Reimbursement Policy (FIN-RMB-001) with department-head approval.

## 6. Special situations

### 6.1 Leave during probation

Employees on probation accrue earned leave from their first month but may use **no more than 6 days of earned leave during probation** without CHRO approval. Casual and sick leave may be used normally. Probation is described in ONB-004.

### 6.2 Leave during the notice period

Earned leave **cannot be used to shorten the notice period**. Leave during the notice period requires approval from both the reporting manager and the HR business partner, and is usually limited to sick leave and emergencies.

### 6.3 Leave without pay

LWP may be approved for up to 30 days in a leave year by the department head, and for longer periods by the CHRO, after all paid leave has been used. Unapproved absence is recorded as LWP. **Three or more consecutive working days of unapproved absence** triggers the absence procedure in the Employee Conduct and Disciplinary Policy (HR-POL-005).

### 6.4 Public holiday during leave

If a public holiday falls inside a period of earned leave, the holiday is not deducted from the leave balance. Weekends inside a leave period are also not counted.

### 6.5 Illness during earned leave

If an employee falls ill for 3 or more days during earned leave and provides a medical certificate, the days of illness may be converted to sick leave and the earned leave restored.

## 7. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Employee | Plan leave early, apply in PeopleHub, keep the team informed, hand over urgent work |
| Reporting manager | Decide on requests within 2 working days, plan team cover, encourage employees to use leave |
| HR Operations | Maintain balances in PeopleHub, publish the holiday calendar, answer balance queries |
| HR business partner | Advise on exceptions, parental leave and long absences |
| Payroll | Process LWP deductions and leave encashment at exit |
| CHRO | Approve exceptions to this policy |

## 8. Exceptions

Exceptions are rare and must be approved in writing by the CHRO through PeopleHub's *Policy Exception* form. Examples of situations that may justify an exception include a serious family emergency after leave is exhausted, extended medical treatment, or a statutory obligation in a specific state. Managers cannot grant exceptions themselves, and verbal approvals are not valid.

## 9. Worked examples

**Example 1 - advance notice.** Ravi wants to take earned leave from Monday 9 November to Friday 13 November (5 working days). Because the leave is longer than 3 working days, he must apply by Monday 2 November at the latest. If he applies on 5 November, his manager may still approve it, but is entitled to decline it for lack of notice.

**Example 2 - carry forward.** On 31 March, Meena has 34 days of unused earned leave. Only 30 days carry forward to the new leave year; 4 days lapse. She then receives 1.5 days on 1 April, giving a balance of 31.5 days.

**Example 3 - encashment at exit.** Arun resigns with 22 days of earned leave. If he uses none during the notice period, 15 days are encashed at his basic salary rate and 7 days lapse.

**Example 4 - sick leave certificate.** Fatima is unwell for 3 consecutive days. Because this exceeds 2 consecutive days, she uploads a medical certificate in PeopleHub when she applies for sick leave.

**Example 5 - manager does not respond.** Karan applies for 2 days of earned leave on Tuesday. His manager has not acted by end of day Thursday, so the request moves to the skip-level manager's queue on Friday morning.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

**Q: How many earned leave days do I get each year?**
A: 18 days per leave year, accruing at 1.5 days per month.

**Q: What is the maximum earned leave I can carry forward?**
A: Your balance can carry forward up to 30 days; anything above 30 days lapses on 31 March.

**Q: Can I encash earned leave while I am still employed?**
A: No. Earned leave can only be encashed at exit, up to 15 days.

**Q: When do I need a medical certificate for sick leave?**
A: For sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days.

**Q: How far in advance must I apply for a week of earned leave?**
A: Earned leave longer than 3 working days must be applied for at least 7 calendar days in advance.

**Q: Can my manager refuse my sick leave?**
A: No. Sick leave, bereavement leave and statutory parental leave cannot be declined.

**Q: How long is paternity leave and when must I take it?**
A: 10 working days, taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption, in up to two blocks.

**Q: Can I use earned leave to finish my notice period early?**
A: No. Earned leave cannot be used to shorten the notice period.

**Q: Which holiday calendar applies if my manager is in another office?**
A: The calendar of your own base office.
