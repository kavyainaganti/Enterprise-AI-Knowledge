# Facilities, Office Access and Workplace Safety Procedure

## 1. Purpose

Veltrix offices should be safe, secure and pleasant places to work. This procedure explains how office access works, how visitors are managed, what to do in an emergency, how to report accidents and hazards, the arrangements for safe travel home after late work, and the everyday rules that keep the workplace secure and comfortable for everyone.

## 2. Scope

The procedure applies to the Hyderabad headquarters and the Bengaluru and Pune offices, and to everyone in them: employees, interns, contractors, visitors and service providers. Business continuity during major disruptions is covered separately in OPS-003.

## 3. Office access

### 3.1 Access badges

Every employee receives a photo access badge on the first day (ONB-001). Contractors receive an orange badge and visitors a temporary visitor badge. Badges must be worn visibly inside the office and must never be lent to anyone. **Tailgating** (following someone through a secure door without using your own badge) is not permitted; politely ask the person to use their badge.

### 3.2 Access hours

Offices are accessible with a badge from **07:00 to 22:00 on working days**. Access outside these hours, and on weekends and public holidays, requires approval from the reporting manager submitted to Facilities through HelpPoint **at least 1 working day in advance**, except in an emergency such as a P1 incident, when the on-duty security team can grant access after confirming with the IT Operations Manager.

### 3.3 Lost badges

Report a lost badge to Facilities through HelpPoint **within 24 hours** so it can be deactivated. A temporary badge is issued on the same day. The first replacement in a year is free; further replacements in the same year cost Rs 500, deducted through payroll.

### 3.4 Office attendance records

Badge records are used to record office attendance under the Hybrid Work and Attendance Policy (HR-POL-002) and for security. They are retained for 1 year and accessed only by Facilities, HR and Security for these purposes.

## 4. Visitors

1. The host registers the visitor in the visitor management system **by 18:00 on the working day before the visit**, with the visitor's name, company, purpose and expected time.
2. On arrival, the visitor shows government photo identification at reception, signs the visitor log and receives a visitor badge.
3. **Visitors must be escorted by their host at all times** in non-public areas, and must not be left alone in meeting rooms with access to company screens or documents.
4. Visitors may connect only to the guest Wi-Fi.
5. At departure, the visitor returns the badge at reception.

Visitor logs are kept for **1 year**. Unannounced visitors wait at reception while the host is contacted. Delivery staff hand items to reception and do not enter the office floors.

## 5. Security and CCTV

CCTV cameras cover entrances, reception, corridors and parking areas; they do not cover washrooms, changing areas or individual desks. **CCTV footage is retained for 30 days** and then overwritten, unless it is needed for an investigation or legal hold (IT-GDL-004). Footage may be viewed only by Security and, for investigations, by HR or Legal with the Head of Operations' approval.

## 6. Emergency procedures

### 6.1 Fire and evacuation

- On hearing the fire alarm, stop work, leave by the nearest marked exit, **do not use lifts**, and go to the **assembly point** shown on the floor plan.
- **Fire wardens** on each floor sweep their area and report to the chief warden at the assembly point.
- Do not re-enter the building until the chief warden or the fire service gives permission.
- Employees who need assistance to evacuate (for example because of a mobility impairment or pregnancy) should tell Facilities so that a personal evacuation plan and a buddy can be arranged.

**Evacuation drills are held twice a year** at each office.

### 6.2 Medical emergencies

Call the reception emergency line and the national emergency number **112**. Trained **first aiders** are available on every floor, with at least **one first aider for every 50 employees** in each office. First aid kits and an automated external defibrillator (AED) are located at reception and on each floor; locations are marked on the floor plans.

### 6.3 Other emergencies

For a security threat, follow the instructions of the security team and notification messages. For situations affecting the whole office or city, the Crisis Management Team may issue instructions under OPS-003, including to work from home.

## 7. Reporting accidents and hazards

- **Accidents and injuries**, however minor, must be reported to Facilities through HelpPoint **within 24 hours**. Serious injuries are reported immediately by phone to the Head of Operations.
- **Near misses and hazards** (for example a loose cable, a wet floor, a blocked fire exit) should be reported as soon as they are noticed.
- Facilities investigates reported accidents and records corrective actions. Reports are reviewed quarterly by the Head of Operations.

## 8. Safe travel after late work

- Employees who leave the office **after 21:00** may book a company cab through the Facilities transport desk; the cost is charged to the department.
- **Women employees leaving after 20:00** are provided a company cab with a security escort or a verified driver, and the route is planned so that they are not the last passenger dropped, unless they choose otherwise.
- Drivers and vendors are verified by Facilities, and cabs are tracked by GPS.
- Employees should confirm when they reach home using the transport app.
These arrangements support the company's commitment to a safe workplace under the POSH Policy (CMP-005).

## 9. Everyday workplace rules

- **Clean desk:** at the end of the day, lock away Confidential and Restricted papers and lock or take your laptop (IT-GDL-001, IT-GDL-004).
- **Meeting rooms** are booked through the calendar; release bookings you no longer need.
- **Hot desks** are booked through the desk booking app on anchor days.
- **Smoking and vaping** are not permitted anywhere in the office; designated outdoor areas are signposted.
- **Food** may be eaten at desks except in quiet zones and meeting rooms with equipment.
- **Ergonomics:** employees may request an ergonomic assessment of their workstation through HelpPoint; Facilities provides adjustable chairs and monitor stands.
- **Accessibility:** offices have step-free access, accessible washrooms and lifts with audio announcements; additional adjustments can be requested through HR.

Feedback about facilities, such as air-conditioning, cleaning or seating, can be raised at any time through HelpPoint under *Facilities Request*; routine requests are handled within 2 working days.

## 10. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| All occupants | Wear badges, follow safety and visitor rules, report hazards and accidents |
| Hosts | Register and escort visitors |
| Fire wardens and first aiders | Lead evacuations and provide first aid; complete refresher training yearly |
| Facilities | Manage access, visitors, transport, maintenance and safety records |
| Security team | Monitor access and CCTV; respond to incidents |
| Head of Operations | Own this procedure; review accident reports quarterly |

## 11. Exceptions

Exceptions to access hours for regular late or early work (for example support staff covering other time zones) can be approved for up to 3 months at a time by the department head through HelpPoint. Safety rules have no exceptions.

## 12. Worked examples

**Example 1 - weekend work.** Ritu needs to work in the Pune office on Saturday to prepare for a customer workshop. Her manager submits an access request through HelpPoint by Thursday.

**Example 2 - visitor registration.** A customer is visiting on Wednesday at 10:00. The host registers the visitor by 18:00 on Tuesday and escorts them throughout the visit.

**Example 3 - late night.** Sameer leaves the Hyderabad office at 22:15 after a release. Because it is after 21:00, he can book a company cab.

**Example 4 - near miss.** An employee notices that boxes are blocking a fire exit on the third floor. She reports the hazard immediately through HelpPoint, and Facilities clears it the same day.

**Example 5 - lost badge.** Aditya loses his badge on Friday evening. He reports it by Saturday evening (within 24 hours) and gets a temporary badge on Monday morning.

## 13. Frequently asked questions

**Q: What are the office access hours?**
A: 07:00 to 22:00 on working days; other times need manager approval at least 1 working day in advance.

**Q: How do I register a visitor?**
A: In the visitor management system by 18:00 on the working day before the visit.

**Q: Can visitors walk around on their own?**
A: No. Visitors must be escorted by their host at all times in non-public areas.

**Q: How long is CCTV footage kept?**
A: 30 days, unless needed for an investigation.

**Q: How often are evacuation drills held?**
A: Twice a year at each office.

**Q: When can I book a company cab home?**
A: When leaving after 21:00; women employees leaving after 20:00 receive a cab with a security escort or verified driver.

**Q: How soon must I report an accident?**
A: Within 24 hours, through HelpPoint.
