# Procurement and Purchase Order Procedure

## 1. Purpose

Veltrix spends a significant share of its budget with external suppliers: cloud infrastructure, software licences, contractors, office services, events and equipment. This procedure makes sure that purchases are needed, competitively priced, properly approved, contracted on sound terms and paid correctly. It also protects the company against fraud, conflicts of interest and supplier risks. The procedure is restricted to managers and functions that buy on the company's behalf.

## 2. Scope

The procedure covers all purchases of goods and services from external suppliers, including contractors (ONB-005), software subscriptions and cloud services. It does not cover employee expense claims (FIN-RMB-001), corporate card purchases within card rules (FIN-RMB-005), payroll, or tax payments.

## 3. Key thresholds

| Rule | Threshold (total contract value, excluding taxes) |
|---|---|
| Purchase order (PO) required | **Above Rs 25,000** |
| Three competitive quotes required | **Above Rs 2,00,000** |
| Written contract reviewed by Legal | **Above Rs 5,00,000**, or any value if personal or Confidential data is involved |
| Formal request for proposal (RFP) with weighted scoring | **Above Rs 25,00,000** |
| Vendor security assessment | Any supplier that will store, process or access Confidential or Restricted data |

**Total contract value** means the full amount payable over the life of the contract, including all years of a multi-year subscription and all phases of a project. Splitting a requirement into smaller purchases to stay below a threshold is prohibited and is treated as serious misconduct.

## 4. The procurement process

### 4.1 Plan and justify

The requester checks that the purchase is in the approved budget and that no existing contract or internal resource already meets the need. For services, the requester writes a short scope of work describing deliverables, timelines and acceptance criteria.

### 4.2 Raise a purchase request

The requester raises a purchase request (PR) in **BuyRight**, attaching the scope, budget code, estimated value and preferred suppliers if known. The PR is approved by the requester's manager for business need before sourcing starts.

### 4.3 Source

| Value | Sourcing rule |
|---|---|
| Up to Rs 2,00,000 | One quote is enough; preferred suppliers should be used where available |
| Above Rs 2,00,000 up to Rs 25,00,000 | **At least three written quotes**, compared on price, quality, delivery and support |
| Above Rs 25,00,000 | **Formal RFP** issued by Procurement, with written evaluation criteria and weighted scoring by at least three evaluators |

Everyone involved in evaluating suppliers must declare any interest in a bidder before evaluation starts, and must step aside if a conflict exists (CMP-002).

#### 4.3.1 Single-source purchases

Where only one supplier can meet the need (for example renewal of a licence for a product already in use, or a proprietary integration), the requester completes a **single-source justification** in BuyRight. Single-source purchases above Rs 2,00,000 are approved by the Finance Controller in addition to the normal approvers.

### 4.4 Approve

Approval of the purchase follows the **Delegation of Authority and Approval Workflow (OPS-002)**, based on total contract value:

| Total contract value | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to Rs 50,000 | Reporting manager |
| Rs 50,001 to Rs 5,00,000 | Department head |
| Rs 5,00,001 to Rs 25,00,000 | Finance Controller |
| Rs 25,00,001 to Rs 1,00,00,000 | CFO |
| Above Rs 1,00,00,000 | CEO, with Board approval |

BuyRight routes approvals automatically. Unbudgeted spend above Rs 5,00,000 also needs the Finance Controller's confirmation of funding.

### 4.5 Contract and purchase order

For purchases above Rs 5,00,000, or involving personal or Confidential data, Legal reviews the contract, including data protection, confidentiality, intellectual property, liability and termination clauses, and anti-bribery clauses for third parties who act on Veltrix's behalf (CMP-001). Procurement then issues the **purchase order** in BuyRight. **Suppliers must not start work before a PO is issued**, except in an emergency under section 6.

### 4.6 Contract renewals

Renewals are new commitments and need approval at the level matching the renewal's total value. BuyRight sends the requester a reminder **90 days before a contract's end date** so that there is time to review usage, compare alternatives and negotiate. Auto-renewing contracts must be cancelled or approved before the supplier's notice deadline; a renewal that happens automatically because nobody acted is still reported to the Finance Controller.

### 4.7 Supplier performance

For contracts above Rs 25,00,000, the business owner reviews the supplier's performance at least once a year against the agreed service levels, recording the result in BuyRight. Poor performance is raised with the supplier in writing, and repeated failures are considered before any renewal.

## 5. Supplier onboarding

Before a new supplier can receive a PO, Procurement completes onboarding:
- legal name, address and ownership details;
- PAN and GST registration, verified against government records;
- MSME registration status, if any;
- bank details, verified with a cancelled cheque or bank letter and a test transfer;
- vendor security assessment, where required;
- signed code of conduct for suppliers.

Ownership details are checked against employee conflict of interest declarations for contracts above Rs 25,00,000.

## 6. Emergency purchases

When an urgent purchase is needed to restore a critical service or prevent harm (for example replacing a failed network switch during a P1 incident), the department head may authorise the supplier to start before a PO is issued. A **retrospective PR and PO must be raised within 3 working days**, with the reason. Emergency purchases are reported monthly to the Finance Controller; repeated use for non-emergencies is escalated.

## 7. Receiving and payment

### 7.1 Goods and service receipt

The requester confirms in BuyRight that goods have been received or services delivered as agreed. Partial receipts are recorded for partial deliveries.

### 7.2 Three-way match

Accounts Payable matches the **PO, the receipt and the supplier's invoice**. **No PO, no pay:** invoices without a valid PO are returned to the supplier. Mismatches above **2% or Rs 10,000**, whichever is lower, are sent back to the requester to resolve.

### 7.3 Payment terms and runs

Standard payment terms are **45 days** from receipt of a correct invoice. Suppliers registered as micro or small enterprises are paid within 45 days as required by law, and never later. Payment runs take place every **Tuesday and Friday**.

### 7.4 Bank detail changes

A request from a supplier to change bank details is verified by a **call-back to a known contact number** from the supplier master record, never to a number in the request itself. Changes are approved by a second person in Finance before the next payment. This control protects against invoice fraud.

## 8. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Requester | Justify the need, raise the PR, write the scope, confirm receipt |
| Requester's manager | Approve business need |
| Procurement | Source suppliers, run RFPs, onboard suppliers, issue POs |
| Legal | Review contracts above Rs 5,00,000 or involving sensitive data |
| Security team | Run vendor security assessments |
| Accounts Payable | Three-way match, payments, bank detail verification |
| Approvers under OPS-002 | Approve purchases within their authority |
| Finance Controller | Own this procedure; approve single-source purchases above Rs 2,00,000 |

## 9. Exceptions

Exceptions to sourcing rules (for example accepting fewer than three quotes when only two suppliers respond) are approved by the Finance Controller and recorded in BuyRight. Exceptions to approval limits are not permitted.

## 10. Worked examples

**Example 1 - laptop accessories.** A team needs monitors worth Rs 1,40,000. A PO is required (above Rs 25,000), one quote is enough (below Rs 2,00,000), and the department head approves (Rs 50,001 to Rs 5,00,000).

**Example 2 - multi-year subscription.** A software tool costs Rs 4,00,000 per year on a 3-year contract. The total contract value is Rs 12,00,000, so three quotes are needed, Legal reviews the contract, and the Finance Controller approves.

**Example 3 - split purchase.** A manager raises two POs of Rs 1,90,000 each for the same training programme to avoid the three-quote rule. This is prohibited splitting; the total of Rs 3,80,000 requires three quotes.

**Example 4 - emergency.** A storage controller fails at 23:00 during a P1 incident. The department head authorises a replacement immediately, and a retrospective PO is raised within 3 working days.

**Example 5 - bank change.** An email from a supplier asks for payments to a new bank account. Accounts Payable calls the supplier's known contact from the master record, discovers the email is fraudulent, and reports it as a security incident.

## 11. Frequently asked questions

**Q: When is a purchase order required?**
A: For any purchase above Rs 25,000.

**Q: When do I need three quotes?**
A: When the total contract value is above Rs 2,00,000.

**Q: When is a formal RFP required?**
A: Above Rs 25,00,000.

**Q: Who approves a Rs 30,00,000 contract?**
A: The CFO (Rs 25,00,001 to Rs 1,00,00,000).

**Q: Can a supplier start work before the PO?**
A: Only in an emergency authorised by the department head, with a retrospective PO within 3 working days.

**Q: What are the standard payment terms?**
A: 45 days from receipt of a correct invoice.

**Q: How are bank detail changes verified?**
A: By a call-back to a known contact number from the supplier master record.
