# Delegation of Authority and Approval Workflow

## 1. Purpose

The Board of Directors is ultimately responsible for Veltrix's decisions, but it delegates day-to-day authority to management so that the company can operate efficiently. This Delegation of Authority (DoA) sets out **who can approve what, and up to what limit**, for spend, contracts, hiring, pricing, write-offs and policies. It also defines the principles that make approvals valid, how authority can be delegated temporarily, and how approval workflows operate in company systems. It is the single reference for approval limits; other policies refer to it rather than repeating limits.

## 2. Scope

The DoA applies to every decision that commits Veltrix financially or legally, or changes how the company operates. It applies in all offices and to all systems in which approvals are recorded, including BuyRight, ExpenseDesk, PeopleHub and the contract management tool.

## 3. Approval principles

1. **Approve only within your limit.** Anything above your limit must go to the next level.
2. **Total value.** Limits apply to the total value of a commitment over its full life (for example all years of a subscription), not to an annual or monthly amount.
3. **No self-approval.** Nobody may approve their own expense, purchase, travel or pay change, or one from which they personally benefit.
4. **Separation of duties.** The person who requests a purchase may not be the only approver, and the person who approves a payment may not also create the supplier record.
5. **Approve in the system.** Approvals are valid only when recorded in the relevant system. Email or verbal approvals are valid only in a genuine emergency and must be recorded in the system within **3 working days**.
6. **Budget is not approval.** Having budget for something does not mean it is approved; the approval steps still apply.
7. **No splitting.** Breaking a commitment into smaller parts to stay within a lower limit is prohibited.
8. **AI support only.** The internal AI knowledge assistant can explain approval rules but cannot approve anything (IT-GDL-005).

## 4. Spend approval limits

These limits apply to purchases, contracts with suppliers, expense claims, travel costs and other spend commitments.

| Total value | Approver |
|---|---|
| **Up to Rs 50,000** | Reporting manager |
| **Rs 50,001 to Rs 5,00,000** | Department head |
| **Rs 5,00,001 to Rs 25,00,000** | Finance Controller |
| **Rs 25,00,001 to Rs 1,00,00,000** | CFO |
| **Above Rs 1,00,00,000** | CEO, with Board approval |

Additional rules:
- **Unbudgeted spend above Rs 5,00,000** also needs the Finance Controller's confirmation of funding, whatever the approver.
- Capital expenditure above **Rs 10,00,000** needs a business case with a net present value calculation at a **12% hurdle rate**, reviewed by Finance before approval.
- Procurement rules (quotes, RFPs, contracts) are in OPS-001.

## 5. Contract signing authority

| Contract value | Who may sign |
|---|---|
| Up to Rs 25,00,000 | Department head together with the Finance Controller |
| Rs 25,00,001 to Rs 1,00,00,000 | CFO |
| Above Rs 1,00,00,000 | CEO, after Board approval |
| Customer contracts on standard terms | VP Sales, any value, within approved pricing |
| Any contract with non-standard liability, indemnity or data protection terms | As above, plus Head of Legal and Compliance review |
| Non-disclosure agreements on the standard template | Any department head |

Nobody else may sign contracts on behalf of Veltrix. Contracts signed without authority may not bind the company and are a disciplinary matter.

## 7. Pricing and revenue decisions

|---|---|
| Up to 10% | Sales Manager |
| 10.1% to 20% | VP Sales |

Changes to list prices require approval by the VP Product, CFO and CEO. Write-off of customer receivables up to Rs 1,00,000 is approved by the Finance Controller, up to Rs 10,00,000 by the CFO, and above that by the CEO.

### 8.1 Combining limits

Some decisions involve more than one table. For example, hiring a contractor through a vendor is both a people decision and a purchase: the business need is approved by the department head, and the purchase is approved at the spend level for the total contract value. Where two approvals apply, both are required, and the more senior approver signs last. A decision with both spend and pricing elements, such as a customer deal that includes a discount and a third-party cost, needs the discount approver and the spend approver.

## 9. Temporary delegation

An approver who is on leave or unavailable may delegate their authority **in writing in the relevant system** (BuyRight, ExpenseDesk or PeopleHub) to a person at the **same level or above** for a maximum of **30 days**. Delegation to a more junior person is not permitted. The delegate's approvals are recorded under their own name with a note of the delegation. Delegations end automatically on the end date.

## 10. Approval workflow in systems

1. The requester submits the item in the system with the value, cost centre and supporting documents.
2. The system determines the required approvers from the value and type, using this DoA.
3. Each approver reviews and approves or rejects within **3 working days**; overdue approvals are escalated automatically to the approver's manager.
4. Rejected items return to the requester with a reason.
5. The final approval is recorded with a time stamp and is available for audit.

Finance reviews the system configuration whenever this DoA changes, so that systems and policy always match.

## 11. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Approvers | Approve only within their authority, check business need and budget, record approvals in systems |
| Requesters | Provide accurate values and documents; never split commitments |
| Finance | Maintain this DoA, configure systems, audit approvals |
| Internal Audit | Test compliance with the DoA annually |
| CFO | Own this policy and propose changes to the Board |
| Board of Directors | Approve the DoA and changes to it |

## 12. Exceptions

The DoA cannot be overridden by any individual. In a genuine emergency, the CEO may approve a commitment above their limit only to protect people, assets or critical services, and must report it to the Board within 7 days.

## 13. Worked examples

**Example 1 - software purchase.** A Rs 3,00,000 annual subscription for one year: department head approval. The same subscription for three years (Rs 9,00,000 total): Finance Controller approval.

**Example 2 - discount.** A sales manager negotiates a 25% discount with a customer. The CFO must approve (20.1% to 30%).

**Example 3 - delegation.** The Finance Controller goes on leave for two weeks and delegates approval authority in BuyRight to the CFO, who is at a higher level. Delegation to a finance manager would not be allowed.

**Example 4 - unbudgeted spend.** Marketing wants an unbudgeted Rs 8,00,000 event sponsorship. The Finance Controller approves (Rs 5,00,001 to Rs 25,00,000) and must also confirm funding because it is unbudgeted.

**Example 5 - emergency email approval.** During a weekend outage, the department head approves an emergency hardware purchase by email. The approval must be recorded in BuyRight within 3 working days.

## 14. Frequently asked questions

**Q: Who approves spend of Rs 4,00,000?**
A: The department head (Rs 50,001 to Rs 5,00,000).

**Q: Who approves spend of Rs 60,00,000?**
A: The CFO (Rs 25,00,001 to Rs 1,00,00,000).

**Q: Does the limit apply to the annual or total value?**
A: To the total value over the full life of the commitment.

**Q: Can I approve my own expense claim?**
A: No. Self-approval is never allowed.

**Q: Can I delegate my approvals while on leave?**
A: Yes, in writing in the system, to someone at the same level or above, for up to 30 days.

**Q: Who approves a 15% customer discount?**
A: The VP Sales.

**Q: Is an email approval valid?**
A: Only in a genuine emergency, and it must be recorded in the system within 3 working days.

**Q: Can the AI assistant approve a request?**
A: No. It can explain the rules but cannot approve anything.
