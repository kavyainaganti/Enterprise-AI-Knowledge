# Relocation and Joining Expense Policy

## 1. Purpose

Moving to a new city for a job is expensive and stressful. This policy describes the support Veltrix provides when a new hire relocates to join the company, or when an existing employee is transferred to another office at the company's request. It also explains how interview travel is handled for candidates. The aim is to make moves practical and predictable while keeping costs fair and consistent across grades.

## 2. Scope and eligibility

The policy applies to:
- **new hires** whose offer letter states that relocation support is provided and who move more than 100 km to their base office;
- **existing employees** transferred to another Veltrix office **at the company's request**, confirmed in a transfer letter;
- **candidates** invited by Talent Acquisition to attend an in-person interview in another city.

Employees who request a transfer for personal reasons are not normally eligible. The CHRO may approve partial support where a personal transfer also meets a clear business need, for example filling a hard-to-fill role in the Pune office.

## 3. Relocation support package

### 3.1 Overview

| Component | L1-L3 | L4-L5 | L6 and above |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lump-sum relocation allowance | **Rs 50,000** | **Rs 1,00,000** | **Rs 1,50,000** |
| Household goods shifting (paid directly to approved mover) | Up to Rs 75,000 | Up to Rs 1,25,000 | Up to Rs 1,25,000 |
| Temporary accommodation | **Up to 15 nights** | Up to 15 nights | Up to 15 nights |
| One-way travel for employee and immediate family | Economy flight or AC 2-tier train | Same | Same |

"Immediate family" means spouse or partner, children and dependent parents living with the employee.

### 3.2 Lump-sum relocation allowance

The lump sum covers miscellaneous costs such as brokerage, deposits for utilities, school admission forms, local transport during the move and small household purchases. It is paid through payroll with the first salary (for new hires) or the salary following the transfer date (for transfers). **The lump sum is taxable as part of salary**, and tax is deducted at source by payroll. No receipts are needed for the lump sum.

### 3.3 Household goods shifting

The company pays an **approved mover directly**, up to the limit for the grade. HR Operations obtains two quotes from approved movers; the employee chooses one. Costs above the limit (for example a second vehicle or very large furniture) are paid by the employee directly to the mover. Insurance of goods in transit is included in the mover's quote.

### 3.4 Temporary accommodation

On arrival, the employee may stay in a company guest house or a hotel booked by the Travel Desk for **up to 15 nights** while finding a home. Hotels follow the limits in the Business Travel Policy (FIN-RMB-002): Rs 6,000 per night in metro cities and Rs 4,000 in non-metro cities. Meals during temporary accommodation are not covered; the lump sum is intended for such costs.

### 3.5 Travel to the new city

The company books one-way travel for the employee and immediate family through the Travel Desk, in economy class or AC 2-tier train. One additional house-hunting trip of up to 3 days before the move may be booked for the employee alone, counting towards the 15-night accommodation limit.

### 3.6 Items not covered

The following are not covered by the relocation package: brokerage or deposits beyond what the lump sum covers, purchase of furniture or appliances, school fees, loss on sale of a property, early termination penalties on an old lease (unless the CHRO approves them as an exception), and transport of more than one vehicle. Moving a pet is covered only through the lump sum.

### 3.7 Vehicles

One car or two-wheeler may be shipped by the approved mover within the household goods limit. Re-registration charges and road tax payable in the new state are the employee's responsibility and may be met from the lump sum. Employees should check state rules for re-registration timelines after moving.

### 3.8 Documents and tax

Relocation support is processed through payroll and Finance in line with applicable tax law. Payroll may treat certain direct payments, such as mover costs, as non-taxable where the rules permit; the lump sum is always taxable. Employees should keep their transfer letter or offer letter, the mover's invoice and travel tickets for their own tax records.

## 4. How to claim

1. HR Operations sends a relocation pack after the offer is accepted or the transfer letter is issued.
2. The employee confirms the move date and chooses a mover from the two quotes.
3. The Travel Desk books travel and temporary accommodation.
4. The lump sum is processed through payroll automatically.
5. Any approved out-of-pocket costs not covered elsewhere (for example a mover's extra packing charge pre-approved by HR) are claimed in ExpenseDesk under *Relocation* **within 60 days** of the joining or transfer date.

## 5. Repayment if the employee leaves

Relocation support is an investment in a long-term working relationship. If the employee **resigns or is dismissed for misconduct within 12 months** of the joining or transfer date, they must repay the lump-sum allowance and the household goods shifting cost **on a pro-rata basis** for the unserved months. Repayment is recovered from the final settlement where possible. Employees whose employment ends because of redundancy or company-initiated restructuring do not repay.

**Calculation:** amount to repay = (total relocation cost) x (12 - completed months) / 12.

## 6. Interview travel for candidates

When Talent Acquisition invites a candidate to an in-person interview in another city:
- travel is booked by the Travel Desk in economy class or AC 2-tier train;
- one night's hotel is booked if the interview schedule needs it;
- local taxi costs are reimbursed against receipts, submitted through the recruiter within 15 days;
- meals are reimbursed up to Rs 1,500 per day.
Candidates do not receive a relocation allowance unless they are hired with relocation support in their offer.

## 7. Approval

| Item | Approver |
|---|---|
| Standard package as per section 3 | Hiring manager and HR business partner (stated in the offer or transfer letter) |
| Support for an employee-requested transfer | CHRO |
| Any amount above the standard package | CHRO, and the CFO where the excess is above Rs 1,00,000 |
| Waiver of the repayment rule | CHRO |

Relocation costs are charged to the cost centre of the hiring department.

## 8. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Employee | Confirm move dates, choose a mover, keep receipts for any approved extra costs, claim within 60 days |
| Hiring manager | Confirm the business need for relocation in the offer |
| HR Operations | Send the relocation pack, obtain mover quotes, coordinate payroll |
| Travel Desk | Book travel and temporary accommodation |
| Payroll | Pay the lump sum and recover repayments at exit |
| CHRO | Approve exceptions and waivers |

## 9. Exceptions

Exceptions may be considered for exceptional family circumstances (for example a family member with a disability who needs specialised transport), international relocations, or moves with unusually high costs. International relocations are handled case by case and always require CHRO and CFO approval.

## 10. Worked examples

**Example 1 - new hire at L4.** Gautam joins the Bengaluru office from Kolkata at grade L4. He receives Rs 1,00,000 as a lump sum with his first salary (taxable), household shifting up to Rs 1,25,000 paid to the mover, up to 15 nights of temporary accommodation at up to Rs 6,000 per night, and one-way flights for himself, his wife and his daughter.

**Example 2 - early resignation.** Jyothi received Rs 50,000 lump sum and a Rs 60,000 mover payment (total Rs 1,10,000). She resigns after 8 completed months. She must repay Rs 1,10,000 x (12 - 8) / 12 = Rs 36,667, recovered from her final settlement.

**Example 3 - personal transfer.** Kunal asks to move from Hyderabad to Pune to be closer to family. Because the transfer is at his request, he is not eligible unless the CHRO approves partial support for a business need.

**Example 4 - extra hotel nights.** Lakshmi has not found an apartment after 15 nights. Additional nights are at her own cost unless the CHRO approves an exception.

**Example 5 - interview travel.** A candidate travels from Chennai to Hyderabad for a final interview. The Travel Desk books the flight and one hotel night; the candidate claims local taxis through the recruiter within 15 days.

## 11. Frequently asked questions

**Q: How much is the relocation lump sum?**
A: Rs 50,000 for L1-L3, Rs 1,00,000 for L4-L5, and Rs 1,50,000 for L6 and above.

**Q: Is the lump sum taxable?**
A: Yes. It is paid through payroll with tax deducted at source.

**Q: How many nights of temporary accommodation are covered?**
A: Up to 15 nights, including any house-hunting trip.

**Q: Do I have to repay relocation support if I leave?**
A: Yes, pro-rata, if you resign or are dismissed for misconduct within 12 months of joining or transfer.

**Q: By when must I claim approved extra relocation costs?**
A: Within 60 days of the joining or transfer date.

**Q: Am I eligible if I ask to move offices?**
A: Not normally; the CHRO can approve partial support where there is a business need.

**Q: Who pays the movers?**
A: The company pays the approved mover directly, up to your grade's limit.
