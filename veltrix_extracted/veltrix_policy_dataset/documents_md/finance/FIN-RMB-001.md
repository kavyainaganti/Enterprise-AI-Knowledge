# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose

Employees sometimes pay for business costs themselves, for example a taxi to a customer meeting or a meal during a business trip. This policy explains which expenses Veltrix reimburses, the limits that apply, the evidence required, how claims are submitted and approved, and how quickly they are paid. The guiding principle is simple: **spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own**, and claim only genuine, reasonable business costs.

## 2. Scope

The policy applies to all employees and interns. It covers day-to-day business expenses and the general claim process. Specific rules for business travel are in the Business Travel Policy (FIN-RMB-002); relocation costs are in FIN-RMB-003; courses and certifications are in FIN-RMB-004; and expenses paid with a corporate card or travel advance are in FIN-RMB-005. Where a specific policy gives a different rule for its topic, the specific policy applies.

## 3. General principles

1. Expenses must be **necessary for company business** and incurred in the course of work.
2. Expenses must be **reasonable** in amount and within the limits in this policy.
3. Every claim must be **supported by evidence**.
4. Claims must be **submitted on time**.
5. Nobody may approve their own claim, and approvers must not approve claims of people senior to them in the same reporting line.

## 4. Submitting a claim

### 4.1 Where and when

All claims are submitted in **ExpenseDesk**. Claims must be submitted **within 30 days** of the date the expense was incurred. Claims submitted between 31 and 90 days after the expense need department-head approval in addition to normal approval, with a reason for the delay. **Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed**, except where the delay was caused by the company (for example a system outage), confirmed by the Finance Controller.

### 4.2 Evidence

- An itemised receipt or invoice is required for every expense **above Rs 500**.
- For expenses of Rs 500 or less, a receipt is still preferred; where none is available, a short description is enough.
- Receipts must show the date, the supplier, what was bought and the amount. A card slip without item details is not sufficient for amounts above Rs 500.
- Where a GST invoice can be obtained, it should be in the company's name and GSTIN so that input tax credit can be claimed.

### 4.3 Lost receipts

If a receipt above Rs 500 is lost, the employee may submit a signed missing-receipt declaration in ExpenseDesk for an amount **up to Rs 2,000**. Each employee may use a missing-receipt declaration **no more than twice per financial year**. Larger amounts without a receipt are not reimbursed.

### 4.4 What to include in a claim

Choose the correct category, enter the business purpose in plain words (for example "Taxi to Infotech Ltd for implementation review"), attach the receipt, and link the claim to a cost centre. For work done for a customer project, also enter the project code from TrackWise so that the cost can be tracked or billed.

## 5. Limits for common expenses

| Expense | Limit or rule |
|---|---|
| Meals while travelling (domestic) | **Rs 1,500 per day** |
| Hotel in a metro city | **Rs 6,000 per night** |
| Hotel in a non-metro city | **Rs 4,000 per night** |
| Use of own car or motorbike for business | **Rs 12 per km**, with start and end locations |
| Local taxi or ride-hailing for business | Actual cost; standard category of vehicle |
| International travel per diem | **USD 75 per day** (see FIN-RMB-002) |
| Client entertainment | Requires **pre-approval by the department head** |
| Team celebration meals | Within the team's approved engagement budget; manager approval |
| Home office allowance | **Rs 10,000 one time**, after confirmation of probation (HR-POL-002) |
| Home internet for hybrid employees | Up to **Rs 1,000 per month**, claimed quarterly |

Metro cities for this policy are Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata and Pune. Hotel limits are per night, before taxes.

### 5.1 Specific situations

**Working late.** When an employee works beyond 21:00 IST at the office because of a production incident, month-end close or a customer deadline, a dinner of up to Rs 500 and a taxi home are reimbursable with the manager's approval. This does not apply to routine late working by choice.

**Team events.** Team lunches, offsites and celebrations are paid from the team's engagement budget held by the department. The most senior person attending pays and claims, so that the approver is not someone who attended. Alcohol at team events is not reimbursed.

**Gifts to customers or partners.** Gifts are reimbursed only when they follow the Conflict of Interest and Gifts Policy (CMP-002) and the Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Policy (CMP-001). Gifts above Rs 3,000 in value require the department head's pre-approval and must be recorded in the gift register.

**Mobile phones.** Employees whose role requires frequent customer calls receive a company SIM; other employees do not claim personal mobile bills.

## 6. Not reimbursable

The following are never reimbursed:
- alcohol, except as part of approved client entertainment;
- traffic fines, parking penalties or other fines;
- personal travel extensions, upgrades or personal items such as clothing and toiletries;
- costs of family members or friends accompanying an employee;
- gym memberships or personal subscriptions (the wellness allowance is handled through payroll);
- lost or stolen personal belongings, unless covered by insurance and supported by a police report;
- gifts to public officials (see CMP-001).

## 7. Approval

Claims are approved according to the Delegation of Authority and Approval Workflow (OPS-002). For most claims:

| Claim amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to Rs 50,000 | Reporting manager |
| Rs 50,001 to Rs 5,00,000 | Department head |
| Above Rs 5,00,000 | As set out in OPS-002 |

ExpenseDesk routes claims automatically. Approvers should act within **3 working days**. After managerial approval, Finance Operations checks the claim against this policy and may ask for more information or reduce amounts above limits.

## 8. Payment

Approved claims are paid with the next salary payment or **within 10 working days of final approval**, whichever is earlier. Payments are made only to the employee's salary bank account. Foreign currency expenses are converted at the rate on the card statement or, for cash, at the RBI reference rate on the date of the expense.

## 9. Audit and misuse

Finance Operations audits a random sample of **10% of approved claims** each month and **all claims above Rs 50,000**. Warning signs include claims just under a limit, weekend expenses without travel, duplicate or edited receipts, and repeated round-number amounts. Findings are discussed with the approver. Deliberate false claims are gross misconduct under the Employee Conduct and Disciplinary Policy (HR-POL-005), and the company will recover the amount.

## 10. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Employee | Spend reasonably, keep receipts, submit accurate claims within 30 days |
| Reporting manager | Check business purpose and reasonableness before approving; act within 3 working days |
| Department head | Approve late claims, client entertainment and higher-value claims |
| Finance Operations | Check claims against policy, process payments, run audits |
| Finance Controller | Own this policy and decide exceptions |

## 11. Exceptions

Exceptions to limits must be requested **before** the expense where possible, through ExpenseDesk's *Pre-approval* option, with the business reason. The department head may approve exceptions of up to 25% above a limit; larger exceptions need the Finance Controller. Typical approved exceptions include hotel stays during major events when no hotel is available within the limit, or a late-night meal when a production incident runs overnight.

## 12. Worked examples

**Example 1 - hotel above limit.** Kavya stays two nights in Mumbai at Rs 6,800 per night because her client's office is far from the company's partner hotels. Without pre-approval, she is reimbursed Rs 6,000 per night. Had she asked her department head beforehand, the extra Rs 800 (about 13%) could have been approved.

**Example 2 - late claim.** Vinod submits a taxi receipt 45 days after the trip. Because it is between 31 and 90 days, his department head must approve it in addition to his manager.

**Example 3 - lost receipt.** Sarita loses a Rs 1,800 dinner receipt from a business trip. She can submit a missing-receipt declaration, because the amount is below Rs 2,000, and it is her first declaration this year.

**Example 4 - own vehicle.** Imran drives 46 km to and from a customer site. He claims 46 x Rs 12 = Rs 552, recording the start and end locations.

**Example 5 - client dinner.** Nisha wants to take a customer team to dinner after a successful go-live. She requests pre-approval from her department head in ExpenseDesk, because client entertainment always needs pre-approval.

## 13. Frequently asked questions

**Q: How long do I have to submit an expense claim?**
A: 30 days from the expense. Claims from 31 to 90 days need department-head approval; older claims are not reimbursed.

**Q: When do I need a receipt?**
A: For every expense above Rs 500.

**Q: What if I lose a receipt?**
A: You can use a missing-receipt declaration for amounts up to Rs 2,000, no more than twice a financial year.

**Q: What is the hotel limit in Hyderabad?**
A: Hyderabad is a metro city, so the limit is Rs 6,000 per night.

**Q: What is the mileage rate for my own car?**
A: Rs 12 per km.

**Q: When will I be paid?**
A: With the next salary or within 10 working days of final approval, whichever is earlier.

**Q: Is alcohol reimbursable?**
A: Only as part of approved client entertainment.

**Q: Who approves a claim of Rs 60,000?**
A: The department head (Rs 50,001 to Rs 5,00,000).
