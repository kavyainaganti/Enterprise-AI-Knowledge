# Learning, Certification and Professional Membership Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose

Veltrix wants every employee to keep growing. Technology, regulation and customer needs change quickly, and the skills that make someone effective today will not be enough in three years. This policy explains the learning budget available to each employee, what it can be used for, how to get approval and reimbursement, how larger investments such as expensive certifications and degree programmes are handled, and the commitments that come with them.

## 2. Scope

The policy covers courses, online learning subscriptions, certifications, exam fees, books, conference registrations and professional memberships that are relevant to the employee's current role or a realistic future role at Veltrix. Mandatory compliance training (for example information security or POSH awareness) is provided free by the company and does not use the individual budget.

## 3. Learning expectations

Every employee should complete **at least 24 hours of learning per financial year**, including mandatory compliance courses. Learning goals should be agreed with the manager and recorded as development actions in the performance review (HR-POL-003). Learning can be formal (a course or certification) or informal (mentoring, internal tech talks, reading).

## 4. Individual learning budget

### 4.1 Amount

Each eligible employee has a learning budget of **Rs 25,000 per financial year**. Employees who join during the year receive a pro-rata budget based on the remaining full months. **Unused budget does not carry forward** to the next financial year.

### 4.2 What it covers

| Item | Covered | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instructor-led or online courses | Yes | Must relate to current or realistic future role |
| Online learning platform subscriptions | Yes | Annual plans preferred over monthly |
| Certification exam fees | Yes | First attempt; see section 6 for retakes |
| Books and e-books | Yes | Up to Rs 5,000 per year within the budget |
| Conference registration | Yes | Travel is covered separately under FIN-RMB-002 |
| Hardware or software for personal use | No | Request work tools through HelpPoint instead |
| Courses unrelated to any Veltrix role | No | For example a cooking class |

### 4.3 Professional memberships

Membership fees of professional bodies relevant to the role (for example a chartered accountancy institute, a project management institute or a recognised engineering society) are reimbursed **up to Rs 10,000 per financial year, in addition to the learning budget**. Only one membership per employee is reimbursed each year unless the department head approves more.

## 5. How to use the budget

1. **Agree the learning goal** with your manager.
2. **Request pre-approval** in **PeopleHub > Learning**, stating the course, provider, cost, dates and how it helps your role.
3. Your manager approves; for items above Rs 25,000 (which exceed the budget) see section 7.
4. **Pay** for the course yourself, or ask HR to pay the provider directly for amounts above Rs 15,000.
5. **Claim** the cost through **ExpenseDesk** under the category *Learning*, attaching the receipt and the pre-approval reference. Claims follow the timelines in FIN-RMB-001 (within 30 days).
6. **Record completion** in PeopleHub by uploading the certificate or a completion note.

Claims without pre-approval may be reimbursed at the manager's discretion only if the item is clearly covered and within budget.

### 5.1 Choosing a provider

Prefer providers on the Learning team's recommended list, because the company has negotiated discounts with many of them and has checked their quality. A provider not on the list can be used if the course is clearly relevant and reasonably priced; include a link to the course outline in the PeopleHub request. Courses that promise guaranteed certification without an exam, or that require sharing company data, are not approved. Group bookings for three or more employees from the same team should be coordinated through the Learning team, which can usually obtain a better rate.

### 5.2 Internal learning

Not all learning costs money. The Learning team runs a monthly calendar of internal sessions, including product deep-dives on Veltrix Insight, Veltrix Flow and Veltrix Connect, management essentials for new managers and peer-led technical talks. Internal sessions count towards the 24-hour learning expectation and are recorded automatically in PeopleHub when attendance is marked.

## 6. Certifications

### 6.1 Exam attempts

The company pays for the **first attempt** at a certification exam. A **second attempt is reimbursed once** if the employee's manager confirms that the employee prepared seriously and that the certification is still needed. Further attempts are at the employee's own cost.

### 6.2 Study time and exam days

With the manager's agreement, an employee may use **up to 8 working hours per certification** for study during working time. Exam days themselves are taken as earned leave under the Leave and Holidays Policy (HR-POL-001), unless the exam is scheduled by the company as part of a customer requirement.

### 6.3 Certification bond

For a **single certification costing more than Rs 50,000** (including training and exam fees), the employee signs a learning agreement. If the employee resigns within **12 months** of completing the certification, they repay a pro-rata share of the cost for the unserved months.

## 7. Larger investments

### 7.1 Courses above the annual budget

A course or certification that costs more than the remaining budget may be approved by the **department head**, provided the department's training budget can absorb the difference. Amounts above Rs 1,00,000 for a single programme also need **CHRO** approval.

### 7.2 Degree and diploma sponsorship

Employees with at least 2 years of service and a performance rating of 4 or 5 in the most recent cycle may apply for sponsorship of a part-time degree or diploma relevant to their career at Veltrix. Sponsorship covers up to **Rs 2,00,000** in total and requires **CHRO approval**. Sponsored employees sign an agreement to repay a pro-rata share if they resign within **24 months** of completing the programme.

## 8. Conferences

Attending an external conference requires manager approval for the registration fee (charged to the learning budget) and travel approval under the Business Travel Policy (FIN-RMB-002). Employees who attend a conference share a short summary with their team within 2 weeks. Speaking at a conference on behalf of Veltrix also requires Marketing approval of the presentation.

## 9. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Employee | Plan learning, get pre-approval, complete courses, claim on time, share what was learned |
| Manager | Agree goals, approve requests, support study time |
| Department head | Approve amounts above the individual budget from the department training budget |
| Head of Learning | Own this policy, manage provider agreements, report learning hours |
| HR Operations | Pay providers directly where requested, track learning agreements |
| CHRO | Approve degree sponsorships and single programmes above Rs 1,00,000 |

## 10. Exceptions

The Head of Learning may approve exceptions for company-wide learning initiatives (for example funding an entire team's certification when a customer requires it), which are charged to the central learning budget rather than individual budgets. Repayment waivers under sections 6.3 and 7.2 require CHRO approval.

## 11. Worked examples

**Example 1 - online course.** Asha, a business analyst, wants a Rs 12,000 data visualisation course. Her manager approves it in PeopleHub. She pays, completes the course and claims it in ExpenseDesk under *Learning*. Rs 13,000 of her budget remains.

**Example 2 - mid-year joiner.** Rohit joins on 1 October. Six full months remain in the financial year, so his budget is Rs 25,000 x 6/12 = Rs 12,500.

**Example 3 - expensive certification.** Meenal's cloud architect certification costs Rs 65,000. It exceeds her budget, so her department head approves the extra Rs 40,000 from the department budget. Because the cost is above Rs 50,000, she signs a learning agreement; if she resigns 8 months after certification she repays 4/12 of Rs 65,000, about Rs 21,667.

**Example 4 - failed exam.** Sunil fails a security certification exam. His manager confirms the certification is still needed, so the company pays for one retake. If he fails again, any further attempt is at his cost.

**Example 5 - membership.** A finance analyst renews a professional body membership for Rs 8,500. This is reimbursed in addition to the learning budget.

## 12. Frequently asked questions

**Q: How much is my annual learning budget?**
A: Rs 25,000 per financial year, pro-rata if you join mid-year.

**Q: Can I carry forward unused budget?**
A: No. Unused budget lapses at the end of the financial year.

**Q: Are professional memberships covered?**
A: Yes, up to Rs 10,000 per year, in addition to the learning budget.

**Q: What happens if I fail a certification exam?**
A: The company pays for one retake if your manager confirms it is still needed.

**Q: When do I have to sign a learning agreement?**
A: For a single certification costing more than Rs 50,000; you repay pro-rata if you resign within 12 months of completing it.

**Q: How many learning hours are expected each year?**
A: At least 24 hours, including mandatory compliance courses.

**Q: Is exam day paid leave?**
A: Exam days are normally taken as earned leave.
