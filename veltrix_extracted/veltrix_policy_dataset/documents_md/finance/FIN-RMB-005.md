# Corporate Card, Travel Advance and Petty Cash Policy

## 1. Purpose

Employees should not have to fund significant company costs from their own pockets. Veltrix therefore provides three ways to pay for business expenses up front: corporate cards for frequent travellers and senior staff, travel advances for occasional travellers, and petty cash for small office purchases. This policy sets out who is eligible, the limits, how spending is reconciled, and the controls that protect the company against misuse.

## 2. Scope

The policy covers corporate credit cards issued in an employee's name and paid by the company, cash and bank-transfer advances for business travel, and petty cash floats held in each office. The rules on what is reimbursable and the spending limits in the Expense Reimbursement Policy (FIN-RMB-001) and Business Travel Policy (FIN-RMB-002) apply equally to spending made with any of these methods.

## 3. Corporate cards

### 3.1 Eligibility

A corporate card may be issued to an employee who:
- travels on business **more than 4 times a year**, or
- is at grade **L5 or above**, or
- regularly pays for approved subscriptions or event costs on behalf of a team, as confirmed by the department head.

Requests are made through ExpenseDesk and approved by the department head and the Finance Controller. The card is issued in the employee's name under the company's corporate account.

### 3.2 Limits

| Cardholder | Monthly limit | Single transaction limit |
|---|---|---|
| Standard cardholder | **Rs 75,000** | Rs 50,000 |
| L5 and above | **Rs 2,00,000** | Rs 1,00,000 |

Temporary limit increases for a specific event or trip may be approved by the Finance Controller for up to 30 days.

### 3.3 Rules of use

- The card may be used **only for business expenses** permitted by FIN-RMB-001 and FIN-RMB-002.
- **Cash withdrawals are not permitted.**
- **Personal use is not permitted.** If the card is used for a personal purchase by mistake, the cardholder must declare it in ExpenseDesk and repay the amount **within 7 days**.
- Purchases of goods or services that require a purchase order under the Procurement and Purchase Order Procedure (OPS-001), meaning amounts above Rs 25,000 from a supplier, must not be split into smaller card payments to avoid the PO process.
- Cards must not be shared or lent to colleagues.

### 3.4 Recurring subscriptions on cards

Low-value software subscriptions (up to Rs 25,000 a year per tool) for a team may be paid with a corporate card if IT has approved the tool through HelpPoint. The cardholder must record the subscription in the IT licence register and cancel it when it is no longer needed. Subscriptions above Rs 25,000 a year must go through BuyRight with a purchase order under OPS-001. When a cardholder leaves, their manager must move any subscriptions to another cardholder before the leaving date, so that services are not interrupted when the card is cancelled.

### 3.5 Reconciliation

Every card transaction must be reconciled in ExpenseDesk **within 10 days of the monthly statement date**: attach the receipt (for amounts above Rs 500), state the business purpose and choose the cost centre. The reporting manager then approves the reconciled statement, using the same approval levels as for expense claims.

If a statement is **not reconciled within 30 days** of the statement date, the card is **suspended** until reconciliation is completed. Two suspensions in 12 months lead to cancellation of the card.

### 3.6 Lost or stolen cards

Report a lost or stolen card to the issuing bank immediately and to Finance Operations **within 24 hours**. Disputed or fraudulent transactions are reported in the same way. The company is liable to the bank for genuine business transactions; the employee may be liable for transactions that result from negligence, such as sharing the card details.

### 3.7 Returning the card

Cards are cancelled on the employee's last working day. Any unreconciled amount or undeclared personal spend is recovered from the final settlement.

## 4. Travel advances

### 4.1 When an advance is available

Employees who do not hold a corporate card may request a travel advance for an approved trip (see FIN-RMB-002). The advance can be up to **75% of the estimated trip cost** for items not booked by the Travel Desk, such as meals, local transport and international per diem.

### 4.2 Requesting

The advance is requested in ExpenseDesk together with, or after, the travel request. It is approved by the same person who approved the trip and paid to the employee's salary account at least 2 working days before departure. International per diem advances can be paid on a company-issued forex card.

### 4.3 Settlement

- The advance must be **settled within 15 days of returning** from the trip by submitting the trip's expense claim in ExpenseDesk.
- Any unspent amount is returned to the company by bank transfer, or deducted from the claim.
- **No new advance is given while a previous advance is outstanding.**
- Advances **not settled within 45 days** of return are recovered from the next salary, after a reminder.

## 5. Petty cash

### 5.1 Float

Each office holds a petty cash float of **Rs 20,000**, managed by a custodian appointed by the office's Operations lead. Petty cash is kept in a locked cash box in a locked cabinet.

### 5.2 Use

Petty cash is used only for small, urgent office purchases where other methods are impractical, such as courier charges, minor repairs or refreshments for a visiting customer. **Individual payments from petty cash may not exceed Rs 2,000.** Every payment is recorded in the petty cash register with the receipt and the requester's signature.

### 5.3 Reconciliation and replenishment

The custodian reconciles the float weekly and submits the register to Finance Operations **monthly** for replenishment. Finance Operations performs a surprise cash count at least once a quarter. A shortage of more than Rs 500 is reported to the Finance Controller.

## 6. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Cardholder | Use the card only for business, reconcile within 10 days of the statement date, report loss within 24 hours |
| Traveller with an advance | Settle within 15 days of return, return unspent funds |
| Reporting manager | Approve reconciled statements and advance requests |
| Department head | Approve card eligibility requests |
| Petty cash custodian | Keep cash secure, record every payment, reconcile weekly |
| Finance Operations | Issue cards, pay advances, audit reconciliations, conduct cash counts |
| Finance Controller | Approve cards and limit increases; own this policy |

## 7. Controls and misuse

Finance Operations reviews card transactions monthly for unusual merchants, split transactions, weekend spending without travel and duplicate claims (where the same expense is also claimed through a normal expense claim). Deliberate misuse of a card, advance or petty cash is treated as gross misconduct under HR-POL-005. Using company funds to give anything of value to a public official is prohibited under CMP-001.

## 8. Exceptions

The Finance Controller may approve exceptions, such as a card for an employee below L5 who manages a high volume of approved subscriptions, or a higher petty cash float for a short-term on-site event. Exceptions are recorded in ExpenseDesk with an end date.

## 9. Worked examples

**Example 1 - card eligibility.** Varun, an L3 implementation consultant, travels to customer sites about 8 times a year. Because he travels more than 4 times a year, he is eligible for a standard card with a Rs 75,000 monthly limit.

**Example 2 - accidental personal use.** Pallavi uses her corporate card for a personal dinner by mistake on 3 June. She declares it in ExpenseDesk and repays by 10 June, within 7 days.

**Example 3 - suspension.** Arvind's statement is dated 5 March. He reconciles nothing by 4 April (30 days later), so his card is suspended until reconciliation is completed.

**Example 4 - travel advance.** Neeraj estimates Rs 20,000 of meals and local transport for a 5-day trip to Kochi. He may receive up to Rs 15,000 (75%). He returns on 12 August and must settle by 27 August.

**Example 5 - petty cash limit.** The Pune office needs a Rs 3,500 repair to a meeting room projector. This is above the Rs 2,000 petty cash limit, so it is paid through an expense claim or a purchase request instead.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

**Q: Who can get a corporate card?**
A: Employees who travel more than 4 times a year, employees at L5 and above, and team members who regularly pay approved subscriptions or event costs.

**Q: What is the monthly card limit?**
A: Rs 75,000 for standard cardholders and Rs 2,00,000 for L5 and above.

**Q: Can I withdraw cash with the corporate card?**
A: No. Cash withdrawals are not permitted.

**Q: How soon must I reconcile my card statement?**
A: Within 10 days of the statement date; after 30 days the card is suspended.

**Q: How much travel advance can I get?**
A: Up to 75% of the estimated trip cost, settled within 15 days of return.

**Q: What is the maximum single payment from petty cash?**
A: Rs 2,000.

**Q: How much petty cash does each office hold?**
A: Rs 20,000.
