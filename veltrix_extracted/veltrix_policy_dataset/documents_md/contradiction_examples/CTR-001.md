# Leave Policy (Version 3.0, 2023) - Superseded

> **LABELLED CONTRADICTION EXAMPLE - NOT AUTHORITATIVE.** This document was superseded on 1 April 2025 by HR-POL-001 Leave and Holidays Policy. It is kept to show how an outdated version can conflict with the current policy. Under OPS-005, superseded documents are never authoritative.

## 1. Purpose

This policy set out leave entitlements for employees of Veltrix Systems Pvt. Ltd. for the financial year 2023-24 and until replaced.

## 2. Earned leave

Each employee received **15 days of earned leave** per leave year, accruing at 1.25 days per month. Unused earned leave could be **carried forward up to a maximum balance of 45 days**. At exit, up to **30 days of earned leave** could be encashed.

## 3. Casual and sick leave

Employees received **10 days of casual leave** and 10 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate was required for sick leave of more than 3 consecutive days.

## 4. Applying for leave

Earned leave of **more than 5 working days had to be applied for at least 3 days in advance** through the HR system. Managers were expected to respond within 5 working days.

## 5. Paternity leave

Paternity leave was 5 working days, to be taken within 1 month of the birth.

## 6. Note for testers

When asked "How many earned leave days do employees get?", a correct system answers 18 days from HR-POL-001 and, if it mentions this document, labels it as superseded.
