Welcome! Some tips from people who joined recently:

- Probation is only **3 months**, so don't stress too much.
- Your laptop usually arrives **within the first week**, so bring your own laptop for the first few days and log in to email from it.
- You have **60 days** to finish the mandatory training modules.
- Don't worry about enrolling your family in health insurance straight away; you can do it any time in the first year.

Have fun and ask in the team channel if you get stuck!
