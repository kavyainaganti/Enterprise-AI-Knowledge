# Draft Note - Proposed Procurement Thresholds (Not Approved)

> **LABELLED CONTRADICTION EXAMPLE - NOT AUTHORITATIVE.** This is a draft proposal that was never approved. Under OPS-005, drafts are not authoritative. The approved rules are in OPS-001 and OPS-002.

## Proposal for discussion

To tighten control over smaller purchases, the procurement team proposes:

- A purchase order for every purchase **above Rs 10,000**.
- **Three quotes above Rs 1,00,000**.
- Department heads to approve purchases **up to Rs 10,00,000**.
- Formal RFPs above Rs 15,00,000.

These changes would need CFO and Board approval before taking effect.
