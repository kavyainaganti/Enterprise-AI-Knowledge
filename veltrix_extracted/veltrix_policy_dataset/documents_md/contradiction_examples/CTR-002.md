# Pune Office Memo - Travel and Meal Limits (Unofficial)

> **LABELLED CONTRADICTION EXAMPLE - NOT AUTHORITATIVE.** This memo was sent by a local administrator and was never approved as an addendum by the Finance Controller or CFO. Under OPS-005 section 7.1, a local memo cannot change company policy. The approved limits are in FIN-RMB-001 and FIN-RMB-002.

Dear Pune team,

Because hotel prices have gone up, please note the following revised limits for business trips starting from Pune:

- Hotels in metro cities: up to **Rs 7,500 per night**.
- Meals while travelling: up to **Rs 2,000 per day**.
- Use of your own car: **Rs 15 per km**.
- You can submit expense claims **within 45 days** of the trip.

Please book directly with hotels if the Travel Desk cannot find something within these limits.

Regards,
Pune Office Administration

## Note for testers

A correct system answers hotel questions with Rs 6,000 per night (metro), meals with Rs 1,500 per day, mileage with Rs 12 per km and claim deadlines with 30 days, citing FIN-RMB-001 or FIN-RMB-002, and flags this memo as unofficial.
